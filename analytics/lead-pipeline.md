## How to Use

- Log every lead after DM/email or comment-to-DM handoff.
- Keep scorecard score as submitted (0–50).
- Track the lowest scorecard section as the first audit hook.
- Mark pilot status only after a successful workflow audit handoff.
- Keep `SaaS fit` as: High / Medium / Low / Revisit.
- Update `Notes` with verbatim language that shaped the qualification.
