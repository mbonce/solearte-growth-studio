## Messaging To Avoid

- [Phrase or angle to avoid] - [Reason] - [Replacement]
- "Guaranteed profit" - Unsupported and explicitly blocked. - "Make buy-list decisions easier to audit."
- "Protect your account" - Account-health/compliance claim requiring human approval. - "Keep risk notes visible in the workflow."
- "Your VA is the problem" - Blames people instead of the operating system. - "Your VA needs approval rules, not vague instructions."
- "This replaces product research tools" - Unsupported competitive/product claim. - "This organizes the workflow after supplier and ASIN research begins."
- "We tell you what to buy" - Implies final purchasing decisions and unsupported decision authority. - "Find gaps in your buy-list workflow."
- "Automate your entire wholesale business" - Overbroad unsupported automation promise. - "Turn scattered wholesale workflows into a clearer operating process."
- "Never miss a profitable product" - Profit and guarantee claim. - "Make supplier review and buy-list confidence easier to audit."
