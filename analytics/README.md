# Analytics

Analytics turns weekly activity into learning. Track output, replies, DMs, scorecard downloads, audits, demos, pilots, repeated pain patterns, product feedback, and next experiments.

Use small-sample judgment. Do not overstate weak data.

## Reporting Standard

Each report should separate:

- Activity: what was shipped or sent.
- Response: what sellers did or said.
- Interpretation: what the team thinks it means.
- Decision: what changes next week.

## Signal Quality

Treat one reply as a clue, repeated qualified replies as a pattern, and closed pilots as stronger evidence. Do not turn weak engagement into claims about the whole market.
