## Problem

FBA wholesale gets messy after sourcing starts working. Suppliers, catalogs, ASIN decisions, buy lists, POs, shipments, replenishment, margins, and VA work spread across disconnected tools.

## Who It Is For

- Amazon FBA wholesale sellers.
- Operators with VAs or sourcing teams.
- Sellers reviewing supplier catalogs.
- Sellers managing repeat buying and replenishment.

## Symptoms

- Supplier follow-ups get missed.
- Catalogs are reviewed inconsistently.
- Buy lists lack decision history.
- Margins change after fees and prep.
- Replenishment is reactive.
- VA work is hard to audit.

## Workflow Map

`[Supplier] --> [Catalog] --> [ASIN Review] --> [Buy List] --> [PO] --> [FBA Shipment] --> [Replenishment]`

## How Wholesale-Ops Helps

Wholesale-Ops organizes the operating workflow around the work FBA wholesale sellers already do: suppliers, catalogs, ASIN decisions, buy lists, purchase orders, shipments, replenishment, margins, VA workflows, and risk-control notes.

Primary motion:

`scorecard → workflow audit → 30-Day Ops Cleanup Pilot → Wholesale Ops SaaS beta`.

Language for operators:

- supplier catalog → verified buy list
- verified buy list → documented replenishment execution
- documented replenishment → better realized-profit review rhythm
