# Commission Page Copy (proposed)

**H1:** Custom Portrait Commissions in Denver

**Intro (live, keep):** Commission a vivid portrait or collaborative artwork from SOLEARTé. Share the subject, desired format, reference images, timing, and where the finished piece will live. Each inquiry is reviewed directly so scope, price, and delivery can be confirmed before work begins.

**How it works:**

1. You tell me who it's for and where it will live.
2. You send a few reference photos. Imperfect is fine.
3. I reply with scope, price, and timing before anything begins.
4. I paint. You get the piece, delivered in Denver or shipped.

**Good for:** milestones, memorials, gifts for parents and elders, self-portraits, collaborative pieces for spaces.

**Form fields:** Name · Email · Who is the portrait of? · Size you're imagining · When do you need it? · Where will it live? · Upload reference photos · Anything else.

**Fallback:** Email solearté@gmail.com to start a conversation.

**Collaborations:** Have a wall, an event, or a product that needs color? Email me with the space, the timing, and what you're imagining.
