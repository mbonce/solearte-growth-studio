# Workflow Audit Page Copy

## Hero

Book an ops workflow audit before your complexity compounds.

## Who It Is For

- Amazon FBA wholesale sellers with VAs or sourcing teams
- Sellers using spreadsheets for supplier, catalog, buy-list, PO, shipment, or replenishment work
- Operators who already know product sourcing is not the bottleneck anymore

## What Happens on the Audit

- 60-minute call to map one workflow in full
- Focused review of source flow and decision history
- Bottleneck mapping across supplier → catalog → ASIN → buy list → PO → shipment → replenishment
- Clear next-step cleanup plan with owner and review date

## What to Prepare

- One week of catalog intake data
- Current supplier list with status
- Current buy-list and replenishment examples
- Current PO and shipment notes
- VA notes or task list

## What They Get After the Audit

- One-page workflow map
- Top three failure points
- Highest-leverage cleanup first
- Pilot-ready recommendation and scope

- Primary: Book an ops workflow audit

- No active supplier catalog workflow
