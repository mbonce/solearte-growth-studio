# Escalation Matrix

## Level 0: Safe Draft

Use for internal drafts, research summaries, non-risky content concepts, and low-risk scorecard CTAs.

Action: specialist agent may draft; QA still reviews public copy.

## Level 1: QA Review

Use for all public-facing Instagram posts, reels, stories, DMs, outbound, landing pages, sales scripts, and lead magnets.

Action: QA approves, revises, or blocks.

## Level 2: Human Approval

Use for pricing, account health, IP complaints, suspensions, legal/compliance advice, angry users, refunds, billing, custom integrations, profit guarantee questions, public customer-result claims, and undocumented capabilities.

Action: do not send or post until human approves.

## Level 3: Do Not Send/Post

Use for profit guarantees, account-protection promises, legal advice, fake metrics, fake testimonials, private user data, copied creator content, and off-positioning copy for brick-and-mortar wholesale or distributor ERP buyers.

Action: block and rewrite.
