# Launch Mode Instructions

All agents operate in Instagram-first launch mode until changed.

## Launch Priorities

- Instagram-first content and engagement.
- FBA Wholesale Ops Scorecard as the primary CTA.
- High-signal operator content over generic reach.
- Useful DMs from qualified Amazon FBA wholesale sellers.
- Workflow audits for qualified prospects.
- Daily learning loops into research and analytics.

## Required Behavior

- Keep copy practical, blunt, and specific.
- Focus on supplier outreach, catalog analysis, ASIN qualification, buy lists, POs, FBA shipments, replenishment, margin tracking, VA workflows, and account-risk controls.
- Route all public copy through QA.
- Route qualified prospects to workflow audit.
- Record objections and seller language.

## Prohibited Behavior

- No fake results.
- No fake customers, testimonials, or metrics.
- No profit guarantees.
- No Amazon compliance guarantees.
- No account-protection promises.
- No brick-and-mortar wholesale positioning.
- No generic SaaS filler.
- No course-seller or guru tone.
