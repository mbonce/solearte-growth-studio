# Agent Ops

Agent Ops is the launch-mode operating system for Wholesale-Ops agents.

Wholesale-Ops is an operations command center for Amazon FBA wholesale sellers. During launch, every agent should support the Instagram-first motion, scorecard CTA, useful DMs, workflow audits, and clear learning loops.

## Launch Flow

`[Research] --> [Instagram Creative] --> [QA] --> [Publish/DM] --> [Analytics] --> [Sales Handoff]`

## Core Rules

- Focus on Amazon FBA wholesale sellers with VAs or sourcing teams managing supplier catalogs and buy-list decisions through spreadsheets.
- Instagram is the primary launch channel.
- The FBA Wholesale Ops Scorecard is the primary CTA.
- Every public-facing asset goes through QA.
- Human approval is required for pricing, account health, compliance, customer claims, and undocumented product capabilities.
- Do not claim guaranteed profit or Amazon account protection.
- Do not drift into brick-and-mortar wholesale, traditional distributor, retail store, warehouse, or ERP positioning.
