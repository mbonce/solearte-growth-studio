# Output Contracts

- Content draft: hook, caption, CTA, alt text, asset list, pillar, permission status.
- DM reply: intent, drafted reply, log entry.
- Proposal draft: template filled except price and dates (artist fills).
- Outreach touch: partner, why fit, drafted message, next date.
- Weekly report: numbers recorded, three recommendations, one experiment.
- QA: pass/fail with fixes.
