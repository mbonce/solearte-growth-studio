# Automation Principles

## Core principles

- Research can be automated earlier than publishing.
- Public publishing requires explicit human approval.
- High-risk DMs require human review.
- All outputs pass through QA and evidence checks.
- No profit guarantee language in any generated copy.
- No Amazon compliance or account-protection claims.
- Store insights and metadata, not secrets.
- Humans approve pricing, account-risk, and policy-sensitive responses.

## Practical implications

- Automations are opinionated but never autonomous by default.
- Escalation queues should keep founder visibility in the loop.
- Every output has a `status` and `risk` field.
- Any unknown claim defaults to `blocked -> human`.

## Quality boundaries

- Never generate fake testimonials, metrics, or customer outcomes.
- Never claim guaranteed results.
- Never suggest integrations or capabilities without documentation.
- Keep every output aligned to Amazon FBA wholesale operators.
