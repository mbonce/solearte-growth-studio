# Workflow Audit Booking Specification

## Goal

Convert qualified leads into a scheduled workflow audit without bypassing sales standards.

## Flow

`[Qualified DM/reply] --> [Intake form] --> [Human confirm fit] --> [Calendar booking] --> [Audit notes] --> [Post-audit summary] --> [Pilot recommendation]`

## Inputs

- Qualified prospect DM or reply
- Scorecard results and pain area
- Team/VA signal

## Steps

1. Trigger intake using `sales/workflow-audit-intake-form.md`.
2. Validate FBA wholesale and workflow complexity.
3. Capture source and pain area.
4. Create tentative booking slot.
5. Founder confirms slot before final send.
6. After completion, write summary from `sales/workflow-audit-summary-template.md`.
7. Generate pilot recommendation with safe framing.

## Automation constraints

- Booking confirmation remains human-approved.
- Pricing and scope follow sales safeguards.
- No promises on outcomes.
