# QA and Approval Workflow Specification

## Goal

Standardize quality gates across public copy and outbound/reply materials.

## Status values

- `draft`
- `qa_review`
- `qa_revision`
- `human_review`
- `approved`
- `blocked`
- `published`

## Review checklist

- Audience fit: Amazon FBA wholesale only
- Positioning: operations-first, not sourcing-only
- Claim safety: no profit/account-protection guarantees
- CTA clarity: scorecard first, audit second
- Instagram-native intent: saves/shares/DM
- Evidence readiness: signal source exists
- Risk classification

## Human approval triggers

- pricing
- account health/risk
- IP complaints
- suspensions
- legal/compliance
- refunds/billing
- custom integrations
- profit guarantee questions
- public results claims
- undocumented product capability claims

## Rejection reasons

- Generic ecommerce guru tone
- brick-and-mortar or distributor-ERP drift
- unsupported capability claims
- fake metrics, testimonials, or case references
- ambiguous target audience
- missing scorecard CTA

## Revision loop

`[Draft] --> [QA review] --> [Fix request] --> [Owner updates] --> [QA recheck] --> [Human approval?] --> [Approved / Blocked]`

## Escalation outputs

- `qa_queue` for revision.
- `human_approval_queue` for risk classes.
- `founder_daily_digest` for blocked items.
