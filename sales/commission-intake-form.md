# Commission Intake Form

Fields (site form or email template):

- Name, email, phone (optional)
- Who is the portrait of?
- Occasion (milestone, memorial, gift, self-portrait, space/collab, other)
- Size you're imagining (small / medium / large / not sure)
- Single subject or multiple?
- Where will it live? (home, office, gift, public space)
- When do you need it?
- Reference photos (upload or attach)
- Anything I should know (colors you love, colors to avoid, the story)
- How did you find me?
