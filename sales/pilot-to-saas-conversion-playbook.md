# Pilot-to-SaaS Conversion Playbook

## Objective

Convert 30-Day Ops Cleanup Pilot outcomes into Wholesale Ops SaaS beta adoption without overpromising.

## When to Pitch SaaS Beta

Pitch SaaS beta after Week 1 audit or Week 4 pilot review when:

- The operator can show the same workflow recurring weekly.
- The pilot removed at least one major handoff failure.
- The team has stable ownership for catalog + buy-list decisions.
- The founder wants repeatability beyond manual cleanup.

## Signals of Strong Fit

- Uses catalog intake + buy-list handoff repeatedly.
- Has at least one active VA or sourcing teammate.
- Already tracks supplier and decision states but with process drift.
- Is already asking for cleaner execution, not only more sourcing options.

## Positioning Wholesale Ops After an Audit

- Name the improved loop:
  - catalog capture → ASIN decision → buy-list approval → replenishment logic
- Emphasize what improved:
  - visibility
  - ownership
  - exception handling
  - consistency
- Frame SaaS as the execution layer for the exact workflow already stabilized in the pilot.

## Convert Pilot Customers into SaaS Subscribers

1. Audit findings are logged in a clean one-page summary.
2. Foundational workflow is mapped and scored.
3. Show one week of what broke without consistent structure.
4. Offer SaaS beta onboarding for the same workflow only.
5. Set review date + monthly optimization cadence.

## Objection Handling

- “I’m not ready for software yet.”
  - Offer a 30-day continuation option with lower scope and SaaS optional.
- “Can it stay manual?”
  - Keep minimum process first; SaaS replaces repetition, not decision judgment.
- “I don’t trust another paid workflow.”
  - Offer a short 30-day scope lock and clear measurable outcomes before expansion.

## Upgrade Paths

- Path A: continue with audit findings only.
- Path B: pilot → one module of Wholesale Ops SaaS beta.
- Path C: pilot → full workflow rollout into SaaS beta.
- Path D: pause and revisit after next scorecard cycle.

## Sample Follow-Up Sequence

### Email/DM 1: Audit Close

> “You cleaned up one high-friction workflow. Your next step is repeatability. We can keep this setup alive in one workflow in Wholesale Ops SaaS beta.”

### Email/DM 2: Value Reminder (Day 3)

> “Your pilot showed gains where decisions were most inconsistent. SaaS beta helps protect that progress so the process survives weekends, vacations, and team changes.”

### Email/DM 3: Pilot-to-SaaS Offer

> “If you are ready to lock this workflow into a repeatable format, we can start the next 30-day SaaS beta onboarding with the same scoring logic and owner boundaries.”

### Email/DM 4: Objection Response

> “No, there’s no all-or-nothing lock-in. We can start with the one workflow you already fixed and expand only if it saves you time and cuts rework.”

### Email/DM 5: Decision Point

> “If you want, we can onboard this workflow into SaaS beta next week, or keep the manual version for 14 more days and revisit.”
