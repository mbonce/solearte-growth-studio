# FBA Wholesale Workflow Audit Offer

## Offer Name

FBA Wholesale Workflow Audit + 30-Day Ops Cleanup Pilot

## Who It Is For

- Amazon FBA wholesale sellers or operators.
- Sellers with active supplier outreach and catalog review.
- Teams already using multiple tools plus spreadsheets.
- Operations currently controlled by handoffs and memory.
- Founders using VAs or sourcing teams for catalog intake and buy decisions.

## Pain It Solves

- Lost supplier follow-up cadence and ownership drift.
- Catalog reviews that never become decisions.
- Buy-list entries with weak rationale.
- Reorder timing based on guesswork.
- Margin assumptions that drift out-of-date after fees, prep, shipping, and storage changes.
- VA actions that create output without accountability.
- Risk notes that are scattered and hard to escalate consistently.

## What Is Included

- 60–90 minute workflow discovery call.
- Workflow map built around:
  - Supplier pipeline
  - Catalog intake
  - ASIN review
  - Buy-list approval
  - PO to shipment handoff
  - Replenishment
  - VA work sequence
- 10-section scorecard section review.
- Billed output:
  - Top 3 workflow bottlenecks
  - Weakest workflow priority
  - Decision trace gaps
  - Suggested operating hygiene upgrades
- 30-day cleanup pilot plan with one primary workflow selected for cleanup.
- Weekly founder check-in structure for cleanup progress.
- Handoff summary for optional SaaS beta onboarding.

## What Is Not Included

- Profit guarantees or account-protection promises.
- Full sourcing replacement, including supplier discovery strategy.
- Legal, tax, or accounting advice.
- Custom software integration work or custom dashboards.
- Ongoing full-time project management.

## Deliverables

1. Workflow diagnosis map (one-page visual + notes).
2. Priority matrix of operational bottlenecks.
3. 30-day cleanup sequence.
4. 30-day success criteria:
   - workflow owner assignments
   - decision quality checkpoints
   - exception routing
   - replenishment guardrails
5. Audit outcome recommendation:
   - proceed-to-pilot
   - extend to next workflow
   - or pause

## Timeline

- Week 0: pre-audit intake and scorecard review.
- Week 1: diagnostic and map.
- Week 2: pilot design and operating protocol.
- Week 3: one workflow cleanup execution.
- Week 4: handoff to founder review and SaaS transition options.

## Price Hypotheses

- Hypothesis 1 (recommended first): project retainer model with capped hourly scope for each pilot.
- Hypothesis 2: fixed-price pilot with a narrow operating deliverable and success gates.
- Hypothesis 3: staged fee for sellers with high operational fragility and larger catalog volume.

## How It Leads Into Wholesale Ops SaaS

The pilot always ends with a practical migration decision:

- What worked.
- What stayed fragile after cleanup.
- Which workflow should become a repeatable SaaS-enabled process.

That transition point is where we introduce Wholesale Ops SaaS beta as the long-term execution layer for the cleaned workflow.
