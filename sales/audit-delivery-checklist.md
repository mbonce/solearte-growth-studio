# Audit Delivery Checklist

## Before Audit

- [ ] Intake form completed in `sales/workflow-audit-intake-form.md`.
- [ ] Scorecard score and lowest section captured.
- [ ] Call objective and scope confirmed.
- [ ] Founder or founder-designated reviewer assigned.

## During Audit

- [ ] Map 10 workflow sections with evidence source.
- [ ] Capture 3 concrete bottlenecks.
- [ ] Validate any risk notes without making compliance claims.
- [ ] Capture exact language from seller around pain points.

## After Audit

- [ ] Complete `sales/workflow-audit-summary-template.md`.
- [ ] Confirm pilot fit recommendation is explicit.
- [ ] Send handoff note to pilot offer creator.
- [ ] Log conversion signals in `analytics/lead-pipeline.md`.
- [ ] Route next follow-up message within 24 hours.
