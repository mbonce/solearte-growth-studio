# Weekly Pipeline Review

Every Wednesday, 20 minutes.

1. Open `analytics/commission-pipeline.md` and `analytics/collab-pipeline.md`.
2. For each open inquiry: what is the next action and who owns it?
3. Anything waiting on the artist for more than 3 days gets flagged.
4. Send follow-ups (one per inquiry, in her voice).
5. Move won/lost, capture the reason in `voice-of-customer/hesitations.md`.
