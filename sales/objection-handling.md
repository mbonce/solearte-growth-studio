# Sales Objection Handling

## We Already Have Sheets

Sheets can be a good starting point. The question is whether the workflow is still visible when supplier count, catalog volume, VA work, and replenishment increase.

## We Use Product Research Tools

Good. Wholesale-Ops is meant to manage the workflow around the analysis: supplier, catalog, decision, buy list, PO, shipment, replenishment, and risk notes.

Use this order to keep positioning clear:

- Run scorecard first.
- Use workflow audit for the weakest workflow.
- Offer the 30-Day Ops Cleanup Pilot for execution.
- Offer SaaS beta only after pilot proof of repeatability.

## Can You Guarantee Profit?

No. Profit depends on many variables. Wholesale-Ops helps organize decisions and assumptions; it does not guarantee profitable products.

## Can You Prevent Account Issues?

No. Wholesale-Ops can support risk-control hygiene and documentation visibility, but it cannot prevent Amazon account issues.

## I Do Not Want Another Tool

If the current workflow is clean, visible, and accountable, another tool may not be needed. If the process is scattered, the pilot can focus on one painful area.
