# Objection Handling (sales conversations)

See `product/objections.md` for the full set. In conversation:

- **Price shock:** acknowledge, explain what goes into the piece (size, layers, time), offer a smaller size or the collection. No discounting reflex.
- **Timeline:** be honest about the queue. A real date beats an optimistic one.
- **Style fit:** show three finished portraits. If they want photo-realism, say this is not that.
- **"Let me think":** "Of course. I'll hold the proposal for [n] days." Then one follow-up.
- **Collab wants free work:** "I'm glad you like the work. My scope and fee are in the proposal; I'm happy to adjust the scope to fit your budget."
