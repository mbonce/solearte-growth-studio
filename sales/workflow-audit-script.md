# Workflow Audit Script

## Opening

Thanks for taking the time. The goal is to map one part of your FBA wholesale operation and identify where the workflow is getting fragile. This is not about promising profitable products or account protection.

## Current Workflow Map

- What tools do you use for suppliers, catalogs, ASIN review, buy lists, POs, shipments, and replenishment?
- Who touches the workflow?
- Where does the founder still have to manually inspect everything?

## Supplier Pipeline Questions

- How do you track supplier targets, contacts, terms, and follow-ups?
- What does approved, pending, rejected, or stalled mean in your process?

## Catalog Analysis Questions

- What happens when a new catalog arrives?
- Who owns review?
- Can you see which ASINs were reviewed and why decisions were made?

## Buy-List Questions

- How does a product move from analysis to buy list?
- Who approves buys?
- What decision history is required?

## PO And Shipment Questions

- How are POs connected to invoices and FBA shipments?
- How do you reconcile shipped versus received units?

## Replenishment Questions

- How do you decide what to reorder?
- What margin, velocity, stock, supplier, and risk signals are reviewed?

## VA Workflow Questions

- What work do VAs own?
- How do you review quality?
- Where do blockers show up?

## Risk/Account-Health Questions

- Where do you keep invoices, authorization notes, restrictions, IP complaint context, and account-health notes?
- Which situations require human review?

## Pain Scoring

Score each area from 1 to 5:

- 1: clean and visible.
- 3: workable but fragile.
- 5: painful and slowing decisions.

## Next-Step Framing

If one workflow is clearly painful and within scope, recommend the 30-Day FBA Wholesale Ops Cleanup Pilot.
