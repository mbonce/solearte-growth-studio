# Discovery Call Script

## Fit Check

- Are you currently selling through Amazon FBA wholesale?
- Are you reviewing supplier catalogs or price lists?
- Do you use VAs or sourcing team members?
- Are you doing repeat buying or replenishment?

## Current Tools

- What is in Google Sheets?
- What is in Gmail, Slack, Seller Central, or task tools?
- What decisions are hard to audit?

## Pain Questions

- Where does the workflow slow down?
- Which spreadsheet is the most fragile?
- What does the founder still have to check manually?
- What breaks when volume increases?

## Buying Context

- Why look at this now?
- What would a cleaner workflow make easier?
- What would need to be true for a 30-day pilot to be useful?

## Next Step

Recommend a workflow audit if the seller has active FBA wholesale complexity and one clear operating pain.

Follow with:

- Scorecard review.
- 30-Day Ops Cleanup Pilot invitation.
- SaaS beta positioning only after a successful 30-day pilot outcome review.
