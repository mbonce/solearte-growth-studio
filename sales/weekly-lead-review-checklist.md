# Weekly Lead Review Checklist

## Input Sources

- `analytics/lead-pipeline.md`
- `analytics/scorecard-funnel-tracking.md`
- `analytics/day-1-metrics-log.md` through `analytics/day-5-metrics-log.md`
- `voice-of-customer/direct-quotes.md`

## Review Checklist

- [ ] Rank leads by scorecard readiness and lowest section.
- [ ] Identify which leads have audit-qualified language.
- [ ] Mark leads needing founder qualification call this week.
- [ ] Remove leads with:
  - no workflow complexity
  - no scorecard follow-through
  - low-quality intent
- [ ] Flag 5 prospects for direct workflow-audit follow-up.
- [ ] Confirm pilot fit where:
  - workflow pain repeats across messages
  - operator roles are clear
  - team is open to 30-day cleanup

## Output

- Prioritized lead list with next action and owner.
- Weekly top 3 objections to feed scripts.
- Weekly pilot-ready leads count.
