# Demo Script

## Confirm Current Pain

Start with the workflow they described in discovery. Repeat the messy handoff in their words.

## Show Supplier Workflow

Show how supplier contacts, approval status, terms, follow-ups, and catalog links can stay together.

## Show Catalog Workflow

Show catalog intake with owner, status, and review progress.

## Show ASIN Decision Workflow

Show how an ASIN can carry cost assumptions, decision notes, risk flags, and status.

## Show Buy List

Show approved opportunities moving into a buy list with owner, approval, and reasoning.

## Show PO/FBA Shipment Workflow

Show PO, invoice, shipment, prep, and receiving context connected to the buying decision.

## Show Replenishment View

Show how repeat buys can be reviewed with updated stock, cost, margin assumptions, and supplier context.

## Show Risk Notes

Show invoices, authorization notes, restrictions, and account-health notes as workflow hygiene, not a guarantee.

## Close With Pilot Offer

Frame the pilot around cleaning up one real workflow from their operation.

Close path:

- If audit confirms one fragile workflow, propose the 30-Day Ops Cleanup Pilot.
- Position SaaS beta as the next step after the pilot outcome is proven for recurring workflow use.
