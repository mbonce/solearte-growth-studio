# Commission Delivery Checklist

- [ ] Final piece photographed (full, detail, on a wall if possible).
- [ ] Permission asked to share the piece and, separately, the story.
- [ ] Care and hanging note included.
- [ ] Delivery or shipping confirmed with tracking.
- [ ] Follow-up one week later: "How does it look in the space?"
- [ ] Ask for a photo of it installed (with permission to post).
- [ ] Log outcome in `analytics/commission-pipeline.md`; quotes to `voice-of-customer/`.
