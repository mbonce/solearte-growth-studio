# Commission Inquiry Script

For DM, email, or in person. The artist's voice.

1. **Thank and open.** "Thank you for reaching out. I'd love to hear about this."
2. **Who.** "Who is the portrait of, and what's the occasion?"
3. **Where.** "Where will it live? That helps me think about size and color."
4. **Size and format.** "What size are you imagining? Single figure or more?"
5. **References.** "Send a few photos. Imperfect is fine, I paint what the photo can't show."
6. **Timing.** "When do you need it by?"
7. **Set expectation.** "I'll come back with scope, price, and timing before anything begins."
8. **Move to email if in DM.** "Easiest to keep this in email so nothing gets lost: solearté@gmail.com."

Never quote price or timeline in step 1–7. Log the inquiry in `analytics/commission-pipeline.md`.
