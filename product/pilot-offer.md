# 30-Day FBA Wholesale Ops Cleanup Pilot

## Promise

In 30 days, help an FBA wholesale seller clean up one core operating workflow so suppliers, catalogs, ASIN decisions, buy lists, POs, shipments, replenishment, and team handoffs are easier to see and manage.

## Ideal Customer

- Amazon FBA wholesale seller.
- Has active supplier outreach or supplier accounts.
- Reviews supplier catalogs.
- Uses spreadsheets and VAs or team members.
- Has messy buy-list or replenishment decisions.

## Included Workflow Areas

- Supplier pipeline review.
- Catalog intake review.
- ASIN decision workflow.
- Buy-list handoff.
- PO and invoice visibility.
- FBA shipment status workflow.
- Replenishment review process.
- VA accountability points.
- Risk-control notes and review flags.

## Success Criteria

- One live workflow is mapped.
- Current bottlenecks are documented.
- Roles and next steps are clearer.
- Seller has a cleaner operating view.
- Pilot produces product feedback and next-step recommendation.

## Out-of-Scope Items

- Guaranteed profitable products.
- Amazon account issue prevention.
- Legal, tax, or compliance advice.
- Done-for-you sourcing.
- Full ERP replacement.
- Custom integrations unless separately approved.
