## Commissions And Collaborations

From the site: "Commission a vivid portrait or collaborative artwork from SOLEARTé. Share the subject, desired format, reference images, timing, and where the finished piece will live. Each inquiry is reviewed directly so scope, price, and delivery can be confirmed before work begins."

Inquiry path: email solearté@gmail.com (also Instagram DM in practice; route to email for scoping).

What the inquirer provides:

1. Subject.
2. Desired format (size, surface, single or multi-figure, portrait or collaborative piece).
3. Reference images.
4. Timing.
5. Where the finished piece will live.

What the artist confirms before work begins: scope, price, delivery.

Rule: never quote price or timeline in public content or automated replies. Invite the conversation.
