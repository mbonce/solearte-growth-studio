# Objections

Honest answers only. If the honest answer is "not yet," say so.

## Commissions

**"How much is a portrait?"**
Every commission is scoped directly with the artist. Ask for subject, size, timing, and where it will live, then the artist replies with a proposal. Never a number in public.

**"How long does it take?"**
Depends on size and the queue. Confirmed before work begins. Ask for their date and let the artist answer.

**"Will it look like them?"**
Her portraits are expressive and saturated, not photo-realistic. Show finished portraits so expectations match the style. The likeness is in the gesture and the color, not only the line.

**"My photos aren't great."**
Ask for a few options. The artist will say what works.

**"Can you ship it?"**
Yes, delivery is confirmed as part of scope. Denver pickup is possible.

## Originals

**"Is it a good investment?"**
Do not sell art as an investment. "Buy it because it moves you and you want to live with it."

**"It's more than I expected."**
Offer the Carry Your Colors path as another way to carry the work, without pressure.

## Carry Your Colors

**"Is the donation real?"**
Yes. 15% of proceeds from the collection support RMIAN's legal services for immigrant families. Say exactly that.

**"What size should I get?"**
Site currently lacks sizing guidance. Ask the artist; add a size note to the product page (tracked in `website/cro-notes.md`).

**"Is this just merch?"**
It is the artist's own artwork, designed by her, made to be carried. Frame it as wearable art, not merch.

## Collaborations

**"Can we use your story in our campaign?"**
Only with the artist involved and approving. The story is hers.

**"Can you do it for exposure?"**
Route to the artist. Default stance: fair pay, clear scope, real audience or impact.
