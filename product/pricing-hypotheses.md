# Pricing Hypotheses

These are hypotheses only. Do not treat them as finalized pricing.

## Monthly SaaS Hypothesis

A monthly subscription could fit sellers who already understand the workflow pain and want ongoing operating software. Pricing should depend on usage, team size, and module maturity.

Risk: too early if buyers still need help diagnosing their operating mess.

## Pilot-First Hypothesis

Start with a paid 30-day pilot that cleans up one live workflow area and proves whether Wholesale-Ops fits the seller’s operating reality.

Risk: requires hands-on delivery and careful scope control.

## Ops Audit Plus Implementation Hypothesis

Offer a paid workflow audit followed by implementation support. This may work for operators who need process design before software value is obvious.

Risk: could drift into consulting unless tightly connected to the product.

## Current Recommendation

Start with pilot-first.

The pilot creates direct learning, reveals actual workflows, and avoids selling a broad platform before the strongest wedge is validated.
