# Product Modules

## Supplier CRM

- Purpose: track supplier targets, contacts, status, terms, follow-ups, and notes.
- Target user: founder/operator, sourcing lead, VA manager.
- Problems solved: missed follow-ups, scattered contact records, unclear approval status.
- Possible fields: supplier name, contact, category, status, terms, next follow-up, notes.
- Content angles: supplier pipeline discipline, follow-up visibility, approval tracking.
- Demo angles: show supplier from first outreach to approved account.

## Catalog Intake

- Purpose: capture supplier catalogs and make review status visible.
- Target user: sourcing lead, VA team.
- Problems solved: uploaded-and-forgotten catalogs, unclear ownership, duplicate reviews.
- Possible fields: supplier, file link, upload date, owner, review status, notes.
- Content angles: catalog chaos, repeatable intake, review accountability.
- Demo angles: show a catalog moving from received to analyzed.

## ASIN Analysis

- Purpose: manage product review and decision history.
- Target user: sourcing team, operator.
- Problems solved: inconsistent criteria, lost notes, unclear approve/reject logic.
- Possible fields: ASIN, supplier SKU, cost, fees, ROI estimate, risk notes, decision.
- Content angles: decision history, ASIN review discipline, analysis consistency.
- Demo angles: show why one ASIN was approved, paused, or rejected.

## Buy List

- Purpose: turn approved opportunities into accountable buy decisions.
- Target user: operator, buyer, VA manager.
- Problems solved: manual buy lists, unclear ownership, stale decisions.
- Possible fields: ASIN, supplier, cost, target quantity, status, owner, approval notes.
- Content angles: buy-list quality control, approval hygiene, operator visibility.
- Demo angles: show ASINs moving from analysis to buy list.

## Purchase Orders

- Purpose: track purchase order creation, approval, invoices, and reconciliation.
- Target user: operator, finance/operator hybrid.
- Problems solved: PO confusion, invoice mismatch, poor receiving records.
- Possible fields: PO number, supplier, status, total, invoice link, received quantity.
- Content angles: PO discipline, invoice matching, clean handoff to shipment.
- Demo angles: show PO from approved buy list to invoice.

## FBA Shipments

- Purpose: manage shipment prep, inbound tracking, reconciliation, and exceptions.
- Target user: operator, prep coordinator, replenishment manager.
- Problems solved: shipment status gaps, receiving mismatch, poor prep visibility.
- Possible fields: shipment ID, PO, prep status, carrier, units, received units, issues.
- Content angles: shipment workflow friction, reconciliation, Seller Central handoff.
- Demo angles: show a shipment moving from prep to received.

## Replenishment

- Purpose: track reorder candidates, timing, and decision logic.
- Target user: replenishment manager, operator.
- Problems solved: late reorders, memory-based decisions, margin drift.
- Possible fields: ASIN, supplier, sales velocity, stock level, reorder point, decision.
- Content angles: replenishment discipline, repeat buying, inventory timing.
- Demo angles: show a replenishables review queue.

## Risk Control

- Purpose: centralize risk notes and review steps for invoices, restrictions, authorization, and account-health hygiene.
- Target user: operator, account-health owner, sourcing lead.
- Problems solved: scattered documentation, missed restrictions, unclear review history.
- Possible fields: ASIN, brand, invoice link, authorization notes, restriction status, risk flag.
- Content angles: risk hygiene, documentation discipline, account-health visibility.
- Demo angles: show risk notes attached to supplier and ASIN decisions.

## VA Workflow

- Purpose: make team tasks, review states, and exceptions auditable.
- Target user: VA manager, founder/operator.
- Problems solved: invisible work, private notes, inconsistent output quality.
- Possible fields: task owner, workflow stage, due date, review status, blocker, comments.
- Content angles: VA accountability, delegation with visibility, process consistency.
- Demo angles: show VA work moving through review without losing decision context.
