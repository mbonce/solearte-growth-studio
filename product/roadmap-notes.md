# Roadmap Notes

## Current Wedge

Start with FBA wholesale sellers with VAs or sourcing teams who analyze supplier catalogs and manage buy-list decisions through spreadsheets.

## Near-Term Learning Goals

- Which workflow breaks first: supplier pipeline, catalog intake, ASIN decisions, buy list, PO/shipment, replenishment, or VA review.
- Which language sellers use when describing spreadsheet chaos.
- Which pilot outcomes feel valuable enough to pay for.

## Product Caution

Do not document integrations, automations, dashboards, or guarantees unless they are real and approved. Keep roadmap notes grounded in observed workflow pain.
