# Brand Brief: SOLEARTé

This is the canonical source of truth for every agent and asset in the repo. Facts below come from https://solearte.co. Do not extend them without documentation.

## The Artist

SOLEARTé is a Denver-based contemporary artist. She is an immigrant. Her work is shaped by identity, migration, resilience, movement, and unapologetic individuality.

She makes:

- Saturated contemporary portraiture.
- Original paintings.
- Custom portrait commissions and collaborative artwork.
- Carry Your Colors, a wearable-art collection.

Her work "translates lived experience into color, gesture, and work meant to be carried forward."

Location: Denver, Colorado, 80211.
Email: solearté@gmail.com.
Site: https://solearte.co (canonical domain on the site is www.solearté.com).

## The Thesis

Movement is natural. People carry where they come from into where they are going. SOLEARTé's work makes that visible, in saturated color, and puts it on walls, on bodies, and in daily objects so it is carried forward instead of left behind.

Short form: **Carry your colors.**

## The Three Offers

1. **Originals**: finished portraits and paintings available directly from the artist.
2. **Commissions and collaborations**: a vivid custom portrait or a collaborative artwork. The inquirer shares subject, desired format, reference images, timing, and where the piece will live. Each inquiry is reviewed directly by the artist so scope, price, and delivery are confirmed before work begins.
3. **Carry Your Colors**: wearable art and objects created "from a simple truth: movement is natural," designed to honor identity, resilience, and the courage it takes to begin again. Current pieces on the site: Carry Your Colors High-Top Sneakers ($109.99), Unapologetic Individuality Mug ($35.00), Unapologetic Individuality Pillow (price not yet set on site). 15% of proceeds from this collection support Rocky Mountain Immigrant Advocacy Network (RMIAN) and their work providing legal services to immigrant families.

## Voice

- First person when the artist speaks. "I designed these pieces to honor..." is her register.
- Warm, direct, unhurried. No hype, no scarcity theater, no "limited drop" language unless it is literally true.
- Color-forward and physical: talk about saturation, gesture, hands, layers, the wall it hangs on, the shoes on the street.
- Identity-rooted without lecturing. She shares her story; she does not narrate anyone else's.
- Proud, not defensive. "Unapologetic individuality" is a stance, not a slogan.

Words that fit: saturated, expressive, carry, forward, begin again, movement, gesture, color, home, resilience, unapologetic, hands, layers, Denver.

Words that do not fit: disruptive, exclusive, elevated, curated (as filler), luxury, investment piece, hustle, grind, content, brand (used about herself), immigrant as a label for other people.

## Visual Identity (from the site)

- Saturated, high-chroma color. Contrast is the point.
- Bold sans-serif display type (site uses Poppins Bold) on a dark header.
- Portraits are the hero. Product shots are clean and multi-angle (the sneakers have eight views).
- Pill-shaped buttons, centered branding, square image ratios in grids.
