## Core Narrative

- A matched catalog row is not the same thing as an approved buying decision.
- Review-state clarity is the message, not automation hype.
- The workflow should preserve owner, status, reason, approval note, and next action.
- The packet should help an operator audit handoffs, not promise profit, compliance safety, or easier growth.

## Safe Claim Frame

- Diagnostic only.
- Workflow clarity only.
- No ROI, profit, margin lift, account-health, compliance, or undocumented product-capability language.
- No invented first-person seller quotes.
- No public-result framing.

## Design Direction

- Visual system: editorial workflow posters, oversized headlines, angled paper-card rows, status language as graphic labels, and strong contrast instead of dashboard-like tables.
- Use fake or anonymized rows only.
- Highlight state transitions: `matched -> reviewing -> needs owner review -> approved -> rejected -> handoff`.
- Show missing context as the problem: no owner, no reason, stale status, no rejection history.
- Avoid polished corporate SaaS-board aesthetics; the direction should feel designed, sharp, and unmistakably graphic.
