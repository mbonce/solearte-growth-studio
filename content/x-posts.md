# X Post Drafts

1. FBA wholesale is not just sourcing. It is supplier pipeline, catalog review, ASIN decisions, buy lists, POs, shipments, replenishment, margins, and risk control.
2. If your VA is the only person who understands the buy list, you do not have a workflow. You have a dependency.
3. A supplier catalog is not analyzed until the decisions are documented.
4. More suppliers do not fix weak operations. They expose them.
5. The spreadsheet is not the enemy. The invisible workflow is.
6. The buy list should answer: why this ASIN, why now, who approved it, what could change?
7. Product research tools help analyze opportunities. Wholesale operators still need process around the opportunity.
8. Replenishment is where wholesale becomes operations.
9. Margin erosion usually hides after the first analysis: prep, shipping, fees, storage, returns, repricing, timing.
10. If supplier follow-ups live across Gmail, Slack, and memory, the pipeline is leaking.
11. FBA wholesale sellers need decision history, not just more rows.
12. Your VA should run a process, not invent one every week.
13. A clean catalog workflow creates a cleaner buy list.
14. Seller Central is required. It is not a team operating system.
15. Account-health hygiene is not a guarantee. It is a workflow habit.
16. The second buy often needs more discipline than the first.
17. If you cannot explain why a product was rejected, you will probably analyze it again later.
18. The operator's job is to make the workflow visible before volume makes it painful.
19. FBA wholesale scaling is less about finding one more ASIN and more about controlling the system.
20. A supplier CRM for FBA wholesale should track status, contact, terms, catalog, follow-up, and owner.
21. Buy-list chaos is usually catalog chaos downstream.
22. The best wholesale operations reduce guessing.
23. If your team cannot audit yesterday's ASIN decisions, tomorrow's buy list gets weaker.
24. Replenishment from memory is a warning sign.
25. More VA hours do not fix unclear approval rules.
26. Invoices and authorization notes should be connected to the product decision.
27. The workflow should show what is ready, blocked, risky, approved, rejected, and waiting.
28. A pilot should clean up one real workflow, not promise a fantasy dashboard.
29. The FBA Wholesale Ops Scorecard should reveal where the operating system is weakest.
30. Wholesale-Ops is built for Amazon FBA wholesale sellers moving beyond spreadsheet chaos.
