## Artist Statement (draft, for the artist to edit)

I paint in saturated color because the world I carry is not quiet.

I'm a contemporary artist based in Denver, and I'm an immigrant. My work is shaped by identity, migration, resilience, movement, and an unapologetic individuality that took me years to stop apologizing for. My portraits and paintings translate lived experience into color and gesture. They are made to be carried forward.

Carry Your Colors, my wearable-art collection, started from a simple truth: movement is natural. I designed those pieces to honor the courage it takes to begin again, and to make sure stepping forward never means leaving behind where you come from. Fifteen percent of proceeds from that collection support Rocky Mountain Immigrant Advocacy Network and their legal services for immigrant families.

If you want a portrait of someone you love, tell me who it's for and where it will live. If you want to carry the colors, the collection is at solearte.co. If you have a wall, an event, or an idea, write to me.
