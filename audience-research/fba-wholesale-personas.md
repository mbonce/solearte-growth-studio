# FBA Wholesale Personas

## Beginner Wholesale Seller

- Goals: understand supplier outreach, open first accounts, learn catalog review, avoid early mistakes.
- Fears: buying bad inventory, misunderstanding Amazon rules, looking inexperienced to suppliers.
- Workflow pains: no supplier tracker, no catalog process, analysis scattered across videos and templates.
- Emotional state: motivated but uncertain, easily pulled toward product-finding promises.
- Content preferences: simple maps, beginner-safe checklists, "what to track first" posts.
- Buying triggers: first real catalog, first VA hire, first confusing buy-list decision.

## Spreadsheet Operator

- Goals: keep suppliers, catalogs, ASIN reviews, buy lists, POs, and shipments under control.
- Fears: losing decision history, missing follow-ups, buying from stale assumptions.
- Workflow pains: too many tabs, unclear status, duplicate analysis, no review states.
- Emotional state: competent but overloaded.
- Content preferences: carousel checklists, workflow diagrams, spreadsheet callouts.
- Buying triggers: catalog volume increases, buy list becomes hard to trust, founder spends hours inspecting sheets.

## VA-Managed Operator

- Goals: delegate sourcing and catalog analysis without losing visibility.
- Fears: VA mistakes, poor review quality, hidden exceptions, founder becoming the bottleneck.
- Workflow pains: private notes, inconsistent criteria, unclear approvals, messy handoffs.
- Emotional state: wants leverage but distrusts invisible work.
- Content preferences: VA workflow posts, review-state diagrams, audit questions.
- Buying triggers: VA output increases but confidence drops, founder cannot explain why products made the buy list.

## Replenishment-Focused Operator

- Goals: reorder winners on time while protecting margin assumptions.
- Fears: stockouts, overbuying, margin collapse, stale fees, competition shifts.
- Workflow pains: reorder decisions from memory, disconnected cost updates, shipment and receiving delays.
- Emotional state: practical, numbers-aware, impatient with vague advice.
- Content preferences: margin and reorder checklists, before/after workflows, short operator reels.
- Buying triggers: missed reorder, bad replenishment buy, unclear updated ROI before PO.

## Scaling Ops-Focused Founder

- Goals: build a real operating system around suppliers, catalogs, team, POs, shipments, and risk hygiene.
- Fears: the business depends too much on founder memory, spreadsheets, and individual VAs.
- Workflow pains: cross-team visibility, process drift, weak accountability, scattered risk notes.
- Emotional state: ambitious but tired of duct-taped operations.
- Content preferences: operator identity content, maturity models, command-center visuals, audits.
- Buying triggers: adding team members, more suppliers, more replenishables, desire to step out of daily inspection.
