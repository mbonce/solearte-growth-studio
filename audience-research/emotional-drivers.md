# Emotional Drivers

What people feel when the work lands, and what the growth system should honor.

| Driver | How it shows up | Content that speaks to it |
|---|---|---|
| Being seen | "I saw myself in it." | Portraits with their subject's story (with permission) |
| Pride in origin | Wanting to show where they come from | Carry Your Colors story, "begin again" |
| Love made visible | Commissioning a parent, partner, child | Milestone commission stories |
| Grief and memory | Memorial portraits | Gentle, unhurried process content, no sales pressure |
| Color as joy | "The colors are insane." | Close-ups, palette talk, saturation reels |
| Doing something that matters | RMIAN 15% | Plain statement of impact, never guilt |
| Courage | Beginning again, individuality | Artist's own story in first person |

## Rules

- Never manufacture emotion with music and slow-motion alone. The story has to be true.
- Grief content gets no CTA beyond "if you ever want to talk about a portrait, I'm here."
