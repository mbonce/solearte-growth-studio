# Replenishment Pains

## Common Pain

- Reorders happen too late.
- Reorders happen from memory.
- Updated costs are missed.
- FBA fees, prep, inbound shipping, and storage assumptions drift.
- Supplier availability changes.
- Competition changes after first buy.
- Stockouts create urgency and sloppy decisions.

## Emotional Driver

The first buy feels like sourcing. The second buy feels like operations.

## Content Angles

- "Replenishment is where FBA wholesale becomes operations."
- "The reorder should not depend on memory."
- "The first ROI estimate is not the final margin story."
- "Your replenishables need a review queue."

## Workflow Fields To Watch

- ASIN.
- Supplier.
- Last cost.
- Current cost.
- Stock level.
- Sales velocity.
- Reorder point.
- Margin assumptions.
- Risk notes.
- Decision owner.
