# Top Pain Points

## Supplier Pipeline

- Supplier targets lack owner, status, and next follow-up.
- Approvals, terms, and contact history live across inboxes and notes.
- Good opportunities go cold because no one owns the next action.

## Catalog Workflow

- Catalogs are uploaded but not tracked through review.
- ASIN matches lack decision history.
- Teams re-review the same products because rejection reasons were not captured.

## Buy-List Quality

- Buy lists include products without approval logic.
- Founders cannot tell whether a VA followed criteria.
- Margin and risk assumptions are separated from buy decisions.

## VA Accountability

- VAs work hard but inside unclear systems.
- Review states are missing.
- The operator has to inspect every row manually.

## Replenishment

- Reorders happen from memory.
- Updated costs, fees, stock levels, and competition are not reviewed consistently.
- Repeat buys become reactive instead of systematic.

## Risk-Control Hygiene

- Invoices, authorization notes, restrictions, and IP complaint concerns are not connected to ASIN decisions.
- Teams discuss account-health risk too late.
- Sensitive issues need human review but are not always routed clearly.
