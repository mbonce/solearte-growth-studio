# Objection Patterns

## "I Already Use Sheets"

- Hidden meaning: I do not want another tool unless the pain is obvious.
- Research question: where do sheets stop working?
- Content response: the issue is not sheets; it is workflow control.

## "My VA Handles This"

- Hidden meaning: I delegated the work and do not want to inspect every detail.
- Research question: can the operator audit VA decisions?
- Content response: a VA needs review states and approval logic.

## "I Just Need More Products"

- Hidden meaning: sourcing still feels like the main constraint.
- Research question: what happens after product volume increases?
- Content response: more sourcing creates more operational complexity.

## "Does This Find Products?"

- Hidden meaning: they may be looking for product research, not operations.
- Research question: are they ready for workflow infrastructure?
- Content response: Wholesale-Ops manages the workflow around opportunities.

## "Can This Help With Account Health?"

- Hidden meaning: documentation, invoices, and risk are stressful.
- Research question: where do risk notes currently live?
- Content response: workflow hygiene, not account-protection guarantees.

## "How Much Is It?"

- Hidden meaning: they may be evaluating seriousness.
- Research question: which pilot outcome feels valuable?
- Content response: route pricing to human review and clarify pilot scope.
