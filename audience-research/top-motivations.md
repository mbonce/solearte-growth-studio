# Top Motivations

Ranked hypotheses for why each audience acts. Re-rank with real data monthly.

## Commissioners

1. A milestone or memorial that deserves more than a photo.
2. Wanting a specific person honored in a style that feels alive.
3. Giving a gift that cannot be bought anywhere else.

## Collectors

1. The color stopped them and they cannot stop thinking about it.
2. The story behind the piece matches their own.
3. Filling a wall with something original from a Denver artist.

## Carriers

1. The name says what they already feel: carry your colors.
2. Sneakers that are actually art.
3. 15% to RMIAN makes the purchase mean more.

## Collaborators

1. A distinctive visual voice for their space or campaign.
2. A story with integrity they can stand next to.
3. Local Denver credibility.
