# Audience Research

This folder turns FBA wholesale seller behavior into usable growth intelligence.

Research here should feed:

- Instagram carousels, reels, stories, memes, and DM prompts.
- Outreach angles.
- Lead magnets.
- Landing pages.
- Workflow audits.
- Product positioning.

## Research Loop

`[Creators] --> [Comments] --> [DMs] --> [Pain Map] --> [Hooks] --> [Assets]`

## Standards

- Stay specific to Amazon FBA wholesale sellers.
- Separate beginners from scaling operators.
- Capture exact language when possible.
- Mark assumptions as assumptions.
- Avoid generic ecommerce, course-seller, and brick-and-mortar wholesale language.

## Output Rule

Every research pass should produce at least one usable artifact: a hook, phrase, objection, content trigger, workflow question, or positioning gap.
