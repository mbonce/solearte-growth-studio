# VA Workflow Frictions

## Friction Points

- VA receives a catalog but no clear review criteria.
- VA marks products as interesting without a decision reason.
- Founder cannot tell which products were rejected or paused.
- Exceptions live in chat messages.
- Review quality varies by person.
- The buy list becomes the first real checkpoint.

## What Operators Want

- Clear review states.
- Required fields.
- Exception flags.
- Approval before buy-list movement.
- Notes that survive handoffs.
- Visibility without micromanaging.

## Content Angles

- "Your VA should run the workflow, not be the workflow."
- "Delegation without review states creates hidden rework."
- "The operator should not need a meeting to understand the buy list."

## Audit Questions

- What does the VA own?
- What requires founder approval?
- What happens when a product is close but uncertain?
- Where are rejection reasons recorded?
- How does a product move into the buy list?
