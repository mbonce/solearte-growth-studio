# Operator Maturity Levels

## Hustler

- Workflow behavior: chases supplier lists, product ideas, and tutorials.
- Tools: notes, browser tabs, simple sheets, Seller Central.
- Pain points: no repeatable outreach, weak catalog review, unclear buying criteria.
- Operational blind spots: thinks more product ideas will fix lack of process.

## Spreadsheet Operator

- Workflow behavior: tracks everything manually in sheets.
- Tools: Google Sheets, Gmail, Slack, Seller Central, product research tools.
- Pain points: tab sprawl, duplicate reviews, unclear owner/status/decision fields.
- Operational blind spots: mistakes "tracked somewhere" for "managed."

## Team Operator

- Workflow behavior: VAs or sourcing team members review suppliers and catalogs.
- Tools: sheets, task manager, Slack, SOP docs, shared drives.
- Pain points: VA review quality, exception handling, approval bottlenecks.
- Operational blind spots: assumes delegation creates accountability by itself.

## Systems Operator

- Workflow behavior: defines stages, fields, owners, review states, and audit points.
- Tools: structured sheets, Airtable, workflow docs, dashboards, operating checklists.
- Pain points: integrating decisions across supplier, ASIN, buy list, shipment, and replenishment.
- Operational blind spots: may overbuild process before identifying the highest-leverage bottleneck.

## Scaled Operator

- Workflow behavior: runs suppliers, catalog review, buying, shipping, replenishment, and team handoffs through repeatable systems.
- Tools: operating dashboards, documented reviews, team roles, reporting cadence.
- Pain points: process drift, training, exception management, margin changes, account-risk visibility.
- Operational blind spots: may ignore emotional load and team trust issues because the system looks mature on paper.
