# Personas

Working sketches. Names are placeholders, not real people.

## "Marisol" — The Milestone Commissioner

Late 20s to 40s, Denver metro. Wants a portrait of her parents for their anniversary. Found SOLEARTé through a reel of a portrait coming together. Worries her phone photos are not good enough and that she cannot afford it. Wants to be told what to send and what happens next.

Best content: process reels, a finished portrait with the story of who it is for, a carousel "how a commission works."
Best CTA: "Send me the subject, size, and when you need it. I'll reply with a proposal."

## "Dev" — The First-Time Collector

30s, works in tech or design, has a first apartment or home with empty walls. Stopped scrolling on a saturated portrait. Has never bought an original. Needs permission to buy art because he loves it, not because it is an investment.

Best content: finished work on a real wall, the artist talking about one color decision, "what it's like to live with this piece."
Best CTA: "Available. DM me for size and details."

## "Ana" — The Carrier

20s to 30s, first-generation or immigrant, anywhere in the U.S. The "Carry Your Colors" name lands immediately. Buys the sneakers or the mug for herself or a sibling. Shares because of what it says and because 15% goes to RMIAN.

Best content: sneakers on feet in the city, the collection story in the artist's words, the RMIAN line stated plainly.
Best CTA: "Carry your colors. Shop link in bio."

## "Jordan" — The Collaborator

Marketing lead at a Denver hospitality brand, venue, or cultural org. Wants an artist with a voice for a wall, an event, or a product. Needs a clear scope, reliability, and a story they can tell accurately.

Best content: a collaboration-ready one-pager, a process reel, a clear "commissions and collabs" page.
Best CTA: "Email solearté@gmail.com with the space, the timing, and what you're imagining."
