# Founder Psychology

## Core Tension

The FBA wholesale founder wants leverage but often does not trust the process enough to stop inspecting everything manually.

## Common Internal Narratives

- "If I could just find more profitable products, this would work."
- "My VA is helping, but I still need to check everything."
- "The sheet is messy, but rebuilding it feels like a distraction."
- "We already looked at that catalog, but I do not remember what happened."
- "I want to run this like a real operation."

## Psychological Hooks

- Control: "Know what stage every supplier, catalog, and ASIN is in."
- Confidence: "Make decisions reviewable."
- Relief: "Stop asking the team for context that should be in the workflow."
- Identity: "Move from seller to operator."
- Discipline: "More volume requires better operating systems."

## Content Use

Use this file to create posts that name the emotional load behind the workflow pain. Keep the tone direct and practical, not therapeutic or motivational.
