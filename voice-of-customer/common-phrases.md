## Cycle 2 Source-Labeled Phrase Clusters

These are public-source research patterns, not all validated direct seller quotes.

- Supplier sheet intake: supplier spreadsheet, price list, product catalog, line sheet, CSV supplier list.
- Catalog analysis workflow: ASIN matching, UPC/EAN matching, bulk spreadsheet scan, upload file, enrich, filter, shortlist, buying decision.
- VA shortlist QA: clean Google Sheets, test-buy-ready, pass/fail decisions, manager-ready review, ready-to-buy flags.
- Approval-state clarity: needs owner review, approved buy, rejection reason, decision history, next action, stale status.
- Current workaround language: manual lookup, copy/paste, third-party tools, spreadsheets, APIs, macros, scripts.
