# Common Phrases

Track repeated phrases that can become hooks, captions, or landing-page language.

## Spreadsheet Dependence

- "Everything is in sheets."
- "Too many tabs."
- "The sheet works until it doesn't."
- "I need to clean up the tracker."

## Catalog Workflow

- "Catalog review."
- "ASIN matching."
- "Price list."
- "Approved products."
- "Buy-list review."

## Team And VA

- "My VA handles it."
- "I need to check their work."
- "The team needs a process."
- "I do not want to micromanage."

## Replenishment

- "Repeat buys."
- "Reorder."
- "Stockout."
- "Updated costs."
- "Sales velocity."
