# Operator Frustrations

## Founder Bottleneck

- "I have to check everything."
- "The team sends me too many questions."
- "I am the only person who knows the context."

## Process Drift

- "Everyone updates the sheet differently."
- "We have SOPs, but they are not followed."
- "The process changes depending on the VA."

## Decision Fog

- "I cannot remember why we rejected this."
- "I do not know why it is on the buy list."
- "The assumptions are not clear."

## Content Use

Turn frustrations into operator-native hooks, not blame. The broken process is the enemy.
