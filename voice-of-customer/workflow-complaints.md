# Workflow Complaints

## Supplier Workflow

- Follow-ups are missed.
- Status is unclear.
- Terms are buried in email.

## Catalog Workflow

- Catalogs pile up.
- Review status is unclear.
- ASINs get re-reviewed.
- Bulk supplier files are too large for one-by-one lookup.
- CSV/XLSX column mapping and identifier matching become a workflow step.
- Supplier files need review surfaces that connect cost, identifiers, restrictions/eligibility, notes, and buy-list decisions.

## Buy-List Workflow

- Approval is inconsistent.
- Decision history is missing.
- Approval state is unclear.
- Rows are approved without enough context to hand off cleanly.
- Margin assumptions are stale.
- Shortlist quality depends on whether the VA captured decision-ready evidence.
- Manager review slows down when the buy list lacks reason, owner, approval state, or exception notes.
- Matched items still need an explicit approved-buy decision before they are handoff-ready.
- Reviewed rows drift backward when the queue stops preserving the decision note, owner, or next step.
- Teams re-open the same review when status survives but the saved reason does not.
- Rows stay ambiguous when the approval note disappears after review.
- Rejected rows get re-checked when the saved reason is not attached to the queue.
- Approved rows get re-checked when nobody can see who cleared them.
- Review status is not enough when the reviewer checkpoint is missing from the row.
- Handoffs stall when the approval receipt disappears after review.
- Rows go back into review without a visible reopen reason.
- Teams repeat the same review when nobody can see what changed.
- Re-opened work stalls when the change trigger is not saved with the row.
- The approval chain breaks when the row loses the reviewer checkpoint, reopen reason, or next owner.
- Teams rebuild the same decision when the context does not travel with the row.
- Rows are not reusable when the decision does not carry forward with the row.
- The row keeps status but loses the decision note.
- The review survives, but the checkpoint does not.
- The team cannot see where the chain broke.
- The next operator rebuilds the decision because the context leaked into memory.

## Shipment Workflow

- PO, invoice, prep, and shipment status are not connected.
- Receiving issues are handled reactively.

## Replenishment Workflow

- Reorder timing is unclear.
- Updated assumptions are missed.
- Decisions depend on memory.
