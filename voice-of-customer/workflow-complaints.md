# Workflow Complaints

## Supplier Workflow

- Follow-ups are missed.
- Status is unclear.
- Terms are buried in email.

## Catalog Workflow

- Catalogs pile up.
- Review status is unclear.
- ASINs get re-reviewed.

## Buy-List Workflow

- Approval is inconsistent.
- Decision history is missing.
- Margin assumptions are stale.

## Shipment Workflow

- PO, invoice, prep, and shipment status are not connected.
- Receiving issues are handled reactively.

## Replenishment Workflow

- Reorder timing is unclear.
- Updated assumptions are missed.
- Decisions depend on memory.
