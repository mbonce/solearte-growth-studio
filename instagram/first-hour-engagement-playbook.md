# First-Hour Engagement Playbook

## What comments to prioritize

1. Real FBA operators who describe active workflow pain.
2. “This is me” comments with operational details.
3. Scorecard requests and workflow questions.
4. Repeat commenters from prior posts.

## When to DM commenters

- DM when comment includes specific pain and asks for next step.
- DM for scorecard requests only after first response in-thread.
- Do not DM generic praise/comments.

## How to identify real FBA wholesale operators

- Mentions supplier pipeline, catalog review, buy-list, PO, shipment, replenishment.
- Uses role terms: sourcing team, VA, operator, supplier rep.
- Describes process volume (catalogs, listings, SKUs, supplier count).

## How to spot workflow pain

- “No one owns follow-up.”
- “Different people interpret catalogs differently.”
- “Cannot tell what was approved and why.”
- “Reordering feels memory-based.”

## How to avoid spammy engagement

- One reply per comment unless user replies back.
- No repetitive templated replies.
- Do not trigger artificial urgency.
- Avoid third-party claims and no fake results.

## How to log useful language

- Capture 3–5 exact phrases verbatim.
- Tag each phrase with context and stage:
  - suppliers, catalog, buy list, replenishment, VA, risk.
- Save in daily metrics note.

## First Hour Sequence

- 0–10 min: confirm publish success and monitor first comments.
- 10–20 min: respond to top 3 operator comments.
- 20–40 min: process scorecard requests and send link-first.
- 40–60 min: review escalation cases and summarize top signals.
