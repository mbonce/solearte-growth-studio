# Instagram Asset Design Packet — Carousel 3

## Final Title

Hiring a VA Does Not Fix a Broken FBA Wholesale Process

## Target Buyer

Amazon FBA wholesale sellers with VAs or sourcing teams managing supplier catalogs and buy-list decisions through spreadsheets.

## Content Pillar

VA workflow management + operational discipline.

## Pain Point

Teams add VAs without clear handoffs, accountability checkpoints, and decision logs, then blame people for process gaps.

## Hook

Hiring a VA does not fix a broken FBA wholesale process.

## Full Copy

1. Hiring a VA is not a process fix.
2. It is only execution capacity.
3. If SOPs are missing, confusion scales.
4. Who owns supplier follow-up?
5. Who decides if catalog stays or drops?
6. Who updates shipment and replenishment context?
7. If nobody owns handoff rules, everyone assumes someone else did.
8. Your workflow should define:
   - Inputs
   - Owner
   - Exit criteria
   - Re-entry checks
9. Use the scorecard to find the highest-friction VA handoff today.

## Caption

A VA can move tasks faster.

But if handoffs are vague, the team gets louder in Slack and slower at shipping.

Good operators use VA support inside a documented flow:
- clear ownership
- documented decisions
- review checkpoints

If this sounds familiar, test your current workflow with the FBA Wholesale Ops Scorecard.

## CTA

Comment “VA” or DM “WORKFLOW” for the FBA Wholesale Ops Scorecard.

## DM Prompt

“Where does your VA handoff break most often: catalog review, buy-list entry, PO prep, or replenishment notes?”

## Visual Direction

- Format: 1080 × 1350.
- Dark operator dashboard look with responsibility map visuals.
- Slide progression from people icon to workflow map.
- Slide 4-6: split each stage by “owner,” “trigger,” “next action.”
- Final slide: scorecard + scorecard link.
- Keep text hierarchy: bold action statement, then short supporting line.

## Design Notes

- Emphasize responsibility vs capability.
- Keep minimal colors (dark background, 1–2 accent tones).
- Avoid “hustle” framing and motivational claims.
- Export `carousel3-slide01.png` through `carousel3-slide09.png` + master proof sheet.
