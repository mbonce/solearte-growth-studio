# Comment Priority Framework

Prioritize comments in this order:

1. Real FBA operators
2. Workflow pain comments
3. “This is me” comments
4. Scorecard interest
5. Beginner questions
6. General engagement

## Priority guidance

- Real operators: reply first with practical question.
- Workflow pain: reply with one diagnostic + scorecard path.
- “This is me”: mirror pain and invite scorecard action.
- Scorecard interest: send clear next step.
- Beginner questions: clarify fit and focus.
- General engagement: acknowledge and keep short.

## Example of escalation priority

- If a higher-priority comment needs founder handling, escalate immediately and queue a brief context note.
