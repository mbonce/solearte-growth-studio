# Response Timing Guidelines

## First-hour response priority

- Operator pain and scorecard requests: first 15–30 minutes.
- “This is me” comments: by 30–45 minutes.
- Beginner questions: by 60 minutes.
- Low-signal engagement: later in same-day review.

## Same-day DM response target

- Scorecard requests: < 20 minutes.
- Workflow pain: < 30 minutes.
- Beginner/operator fit question: < 90 minutes.
- Escalation review queue: immediate.

## Escalation timing

- Pricing/account-health/demo/integration/profit guarantee questions: within 30 minutes.
- Spam/abusive messages: immediate safe closure.

## Founder-only response timing

- Founder must respond same day to:
  - pricing
  - account-health
  - demo booking details
  - compliance or legal risk
  - angry/inflammatory concerns
  - integration promises
