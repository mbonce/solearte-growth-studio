# Reel Hooks

See `creative-research/high-performing-hooks.md` for the full set. Top five to start:

1. "I don't paint the photo. I paint what the photo can't show."
2. "A painting you can walk in."
3. "Eyes last. Always."
4. "Movement is natural. That's the whole collection."
5. "Send me the photo you think isn't good enough."
