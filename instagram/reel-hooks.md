# Reel Hooks

Reels should sound like an operator explaining the mess plainly, not a course seller doing hype.

## Week 1 Reel 1: FBA Wholesale Is Not Just Sourcing

- Hook: FBA wholesale is not just sourcing.
- On-screen text: "More sourcing volume creates more operations."
- Voiceover angle: finding products is only the start; the operation has to manage suppliers, catalogs, ASIN decisions, buy lists, POs, FBA shipments, replenishment, margins, and VA workflow.
- Shot direction: talking head, messy spreadsheet mockup, workflow diagram, scorecard CTA.
- CTA: Comment "scorecard."
- Length: 35-45 seconds.

## Week 1 Reel 2: Catalog-To-Buy-List Handoff

- Hook: The catalog-to-buy-list handoff is where chaos starts.
- On-screen text: "Matched does not mean approved."
- Voiceover angle: a catalog can be matched to ASINs and still lack decision history, approval logic, risk notes, and margin assumptions.
- Shot direction: whiteboard flow from Catalog -> ASIN Review -> Buy List, then add missing fields.
- CTA: DM "BUY LIST."
- Length: 30-40 seconds.

## Additional Hook Bank

1. If your VA found the product but you cannot see why, your workflow is broken.
2. A supplier catalog sitting in Drive is not a pipeline.
3. The buy list should not be a mystery file.
4. The first ROI estimate is not the final margin story.
5. Replenishment is where FBA wholesale becomes operations.
6. Supplier follow-ups should not depend on memory.
7. Your spreadsheet is tracking data, but is it enforcing workflow?
8. More ASINs can create more confusion if decisions are not documented.
9. The founder should not be the only person who knows why a product was approved.
10. Your VA needs a workflow, not just a task list.

## Reel QA

- Hook is clear in the first two seconds.
- No claims about guaranteed profit.
- No Amazon account-protection promises.
- Clear CTA to scorecard, DM, or workflow audit.
