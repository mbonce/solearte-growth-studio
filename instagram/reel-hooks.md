## Starter Reel Concepts

### Reel 1: Catalog Review Is Not Done

- Hook: A supplier catalog is not analyzed until the decisions are documented.
- Pain example: the file was reviewed, but nobody knows which ASINs were rejected or why.
- Operator principle: catalog review needs status, owner, and decision history.
- Practical fix: require approve, reject, pause, or revisit on every reviewed ASIN.
- CTA: DM "OPS" for the FBA Wholesale Ops Scorecard.

### Reel 2: Your VA Is Not The System

- Hook: Your VA should not be your operating system.
- Pain example: the VA knows the notes, but the operator cannot audit the decision.
- Operator principle: delegation needs review states and approval rules.
- Practical fix: separate found, reviewed, approved, rejected, and buy-list-ready.
- CTA: Save this before assigning the next catalog.

### Reel 3: Replenishment Test

- Hook: The reorder is the real operator test.
- Pain example: the first buy looked good, but costs, fees, stock, and competition changed.
- Operator principle: replenishment needs updated assumptions.
- Practical fix: review cost, stock, velocity, margin, and risk notes before reordering.
- CTA: Comment "scorecard" if replenishment is still manual.
