## What To Capture

- Repeated seller pain in comments.
- Carousel formats that get saves.
- Reel hooks that trigger relevant comments.
- Story prompts that would produce qualified DMs.
- Gaps around operations, VA accountability, catalog workflow, and replenishment.

## What To Avoid Copying

- Income claims.
- Guru positioning.
- Private screenshots.
- Amazon policy advice.
- Claims about account protection.
- Generic ecommerce templates.

## Research Questions

- Which accounts already own "Amazon wholesale sourcing"?
- Who talks about operations after sourcing?
- Which posts produce comments from real FBA wholesale sellers?
- What language do sellers use when complaining about spreadsheets, VAs, catalogs, and buy lists?
