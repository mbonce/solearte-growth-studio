# Instagram Asset Design Packet — Reel 1

## Final Title

FBA wholesale is not just sourcing. It is operations.

## Target Buyer

Amazon FBA wholesale sellers with VAs or sourcing teams managing supplier catalog-to-buy processes in spreadsheets.

## Content Pillar

FBA wholesale ops discipline.

## Pain Point

Handoffs are not reviewable once decisions move from catalog review to buy-list execution.

## Hook

FBA wholesale is not just sourcing. It is operations.

## Full Copy

**On-screen script:**

1. “FBA wholesale is not just sourcing.”
2. “Sourcing creates more rows.”
3. “The next leak is usually the handoff.”
4. “Supplier → Catalog → ASIN → Buy List → PO → Shipment → Replenishment.”
5. “If owner, status, and reason are missing, one bad handoff becomes six problems.”
6. “The scorecard helps you identify the first weak workflow.”
7. “Comment scorecard or DM OPS to get it.”

## Caption

More sourcing volume can widen the same handoff weakness.

If the team cannot explain who owns each catalog move, why each ASIN got approved, or how replenishment decisions are documented, the operator gets stretched thin.

Start with a short diagnostic instead of another product hunt.

Comment “scorecard” or DM “OPS” for the FBA Wholesale Ops Scorecard.

## CTA

Comment “scorecard” or DM “OPS.”

## DM Prompt

“After you run it, send me your lowest-scoring section: suppliers, catalogs, buy list, shipments, replenishment, VA workflow, or risk control.”

## Visual Direction

- Format: 1080 × 1920 (vertical).
- Open with founder to camera, then show anonymized workflow visuals.
- Whiteboard/digital overlay for the handoff chain.
- End card includes scorecard CTA and placeholder link.
- Keep frame-safe typography in center 70% of viewport.

## Design Notes

- Keep edits minimal, fast cuts every 6–8 seconds.
- Use dark neutral palette with muted contrast accents.
- No stock music drops that distract from process clarity.
- Export MP4, 30 fps, H.264 baseline, max width suitable for Instagram.

## QA Checklist
