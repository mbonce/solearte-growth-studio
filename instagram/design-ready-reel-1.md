# Design-Ready Spec: Reel 1

## Topic

FBA wholesale is not just sourcing. It is operations.

## Hook

FBA wholesale is not just sourcing.  
Most chaos starts after the catalog gets “matched.”

## Voiceover

“Most teams start by improving sourcing.  
Then the workflow breaks after purchase intent.
Who owns the supplier follow-up?  
Where is catalog review documented?  
Why did this ASIN make the buy list?  
What changed before replenishment?”

“This is why FBA wholesale winners scale operations first, not only sourcing.”

## Shot List

1. **Shot 1 (0:00–0:04):** Founder-to-camera opener, direct statement of the thesis.
2. **Shot 2 (0:05–0:10):** Tight shot of a spreadsheet with highlighted duplicate rows and mixed statuses.
3. **Shot 3 (0:11–0:18):** Screen capture of catalog review list, then ASIN decision list, then buy list.
4. **Shot 4 (0:19–0:26):** Whiteboard/digital overlay showing flow: Supplier → Catalog → ASIN → Buy List → PO → FBA Shipment → Replenishment.
5. **Shot 5 (0:27–0:33):** Founder recap + CTA overlay and scorecard link placeholder.

## On-Screen Text by Timestamp

- 0:00: “FBA wholesale is not just sourcing.”
- 0:05: “Sourcing growth without ops discipline creates chaos.”
- 0:11: “Supplier + catalog + ASIN + buy list must be linked.”
- 0:19: “Is there owner, status, and reason at each handoff?”
- 0:27: “Get the FBA Wholesale Ops Scorecard.”

## Caption

If you are tracking suppliers, catalogs, and buy-list decisions only in notes or spreadsheets, you are probably one operational bottleneck away from a bigger cleanup.

FBA wholesale is not about finding more products.

It is about making supplier, catalog, and replenishment decisions reviewable.

Comment “scorecard” or DM “OPS” and I will send the scorecard.

## CTA

Comment “scorecard” or DM “OPS.”

## B-Roll / Screen Direction

- Use a neutral, screen-record style look for operational credibility.
- Show realistic but anonymized workflow visuals (no sensitive data).
- Keep movement simple and readable.
- Use white pointer lines to show handoff movement.

## Suggested Length

30–45 seconds

## Export Checklist

- Resolution: 1080 × 1920
- Frame-safe text for mobile center area only.
- Keep captions and subtitles readable at first second.
- Final file: MP4, 30 fps.
- Add end card with CTA and scorecard placeholder.
- No music only if it hides voiceover.
- Alt text (if possible in CMS): “Reel about fixing FBA wholesale workflow links between supplier, catalog, ASIN, and buy list.”
