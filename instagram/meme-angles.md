# Meme Angles

Memes should make FBA wholesale sellers feel seen without becoming guru content.

## Safe Meme Themes

- The supplier catalog that "only needs a quick review."
- The buy list with no decision notes.
- The VA who knows the workflow better than the founder.
- The reorder decision based on memory.
- The spreadsheet with 47 tabs.
- The supplier follow-up buried in Gmail.
- The ASIN that keeps getting re-reviewed because nobody documented the first decision.
- The ROI estimate before prep, shipping, storage, and repricing.
- The shipment issue that nobody connected back to the PO.
- The founder trying to scale sourcing before workflow.

## Caption Patterns

- "FBA wholesale sellers know this one."
- "This is not a sourcing problem. This is an ops problem."
- "Save the catalog before it becomes another mystery tab."
- "Your buy list deserves a paper trail."
- "The VA is not the workflow."

## Boundaries

- Do not mock specific sellers.
- Do not use real screenshots without permission.
- Do not imply guaranteed profit or account protection.
- Do not turn Amazon policy issues into jokes.
