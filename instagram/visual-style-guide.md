# Instagram Visual Style Guide

From the site: saturated color, bold sans-serif (Poppins), dark header, portraits hero, square grids, pill buttons.

- Reels 9:16, feed 4:5 or 1:1.
- Text overlays: bold sans-serif, white or black, three to six words.
- Color: let the work be the color; backgrounds neutral.
- Product: sneakers on feet in the city; mug and pillow in real rooms.
- Face and hands appear often. The artist is the account.
- No stock footage, no AI-generated art, no filters that shift the palette.
