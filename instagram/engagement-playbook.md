# Engagement Playbook

- First 60 minutes after posting: reply to every comment in the artist's voice.
- Daily: 15 minutes commenting on adjacent artists, Denver accounts, and followers' posts. Real sentences, no emoji-only.
- Weekly: DM three people who saved or shared the collection story with a thank-you, no pitch.
- Monthly: repost followers' photos wearing Carry Your Colors, with permission.

Never: engagement pods, comment bots, mass follow/unfollow.
