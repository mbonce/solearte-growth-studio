# Instagram Engagement Playbook

## Replying To Comments

- Reply within the first 20-30 minutes after posting.
- Ask one useful workflow question.
- Keep replies short.
- Pull repeated phrases into `voice-of-customer/`.
- Do not argue with off-topic comments.

## When To DM

DM when:
- Someone asks for the scorecard.
- Someone names a specific workflow pain.
- Someone says "this is me" and appears to fit FBA wholesale.
- Someone asks for a demo or audit.

Do not DM aggressively from generic likes.

## When To Ask A Question

Ask a question when the fit is unclear.

Good questions:
- "Are you currently doing FBA wholesale?"
- "Do you review supplier catalogs?"
- "Do VAs help with catalog review?"
- "Which workflow is messiest?"

## When To Send Scorecard

Send scorecard when:
- They request it.
- They confirm active FBA wholesale workflow.
- They mention suppliers, catalogs, buy lists, VAs, or replenishment.

## When To Route To Workflow Audit

Route to audit when:
- They describe active workflow pain.
- They use VAs or sourcing team.
- Catalog-to-buy-list handoff is messy.
- Replenishment or PO/shipment workflow is causing operational drag.
- They ask for demo and appear qualified.

## When To Escalate To Human

Escalate:
- Pricing.
- Account health.
- IP complaints.
- Suspensions.
- Legal/compliance advice.
- Angry users.
- Refunds.
- Billing.
- Custom integrations.
- Profit guarantee questions.
- Product capability questions not documented.

## How To Log Seller Pain Points

Record:
- Exact phrase.
- Source: comment or DM.
- Workflow area.
- Persona or maturity level.
- Next content opportunity.
- Whether it suggests scorecard, audit, or product positioning update.
