# Manual Posting Workflow

This is the manual execution loop for Week 1 launch.

`asset ready` --> `QA` --> `founder approval` --> `export` --> `publish` --> `story repost` --> `monitor comments` --> `monitor DMs` --> `capture metrics` --> `update insights`

## Step 1: Asset ready

- Confirm correct asset file is present (`design-ready-*.md`).
- Confirm linked story sequence is available.
- Confirm target hook and CTA match Week 1 plan.

## Step 2: QA

- Copy is Instagram-native and practical.
- No unsupported claims on profit/account protection.
- One clear scorecard CTA exists.
- No brick-and-mortar/distributor framing.

## Step 3: Founder approval

- Founder signs off on final caption and visual.
- Founder confirms no account-risk or compliance promises.

## Step 4: Export

- Export final asset files with correct dimensions.
- Verify readable text at phone size.
- Save files with deterministic naming.

## Step 5: Publish

- Post at planned time.
- Confirm posting succeeded and post URL is captured.

## Step 6: Story repost

- Publish planned story sequence in the same window.
- Keep each story frame aligned to first-hour questions and CTA.

## Step 7: Monitor comments

- Answer comments by priority (real operators first).
- Keep replies brief and practical.

## Step 8: Monitor DMs

- Route scorecard requests with link-first workflow.
- Escalate high-risk questions to founder.

## Step 9: Capture metrics

- Log all launch metrics in the daily metrics file.
- Confirm scorecard clicks and request count.

## Step 10: Update insights

- Add 5 strongest phrases to `voice-of-customer/direct-quotes.md`.
- Add top hook signal and pain notes to `launch/launch-command-center.md`.

## Completion check

- [ ] Today’s asset posted with story.
- [ ] Metrics logged.
- [ ] Founder escalation list up to date.
- [ ] Day next actions documented.
