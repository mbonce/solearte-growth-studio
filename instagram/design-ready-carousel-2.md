# Instagram Asset Design Packet — Carousel 2

## Final Title

A Supplier Catalog Is Not an Opportunity Until It Has Workflow

## Target Buyer

Amazon FBA wholesale sellers with VAs or sourcing teams managing supplier catalogs and buy-list decisions through spreadsheets.

## Content Pillar

Supplier pipeline management.

## Pain Point

Supplier catalogs get imported and reviewed, but decisions, ownership, and follow-up state are inconsistent.

## Hook

A supplier catalog is not an opportunity until the workflow is in place.

## Full Copy

1. A supplier catalog is not an opportunity yet.
2. It is only an opportunity if someone owns it end-to-end.
3. Who found it? Who validated it? Who approved it?
4. How long has it sat before decision?
5. Why did it move from inbox to buy list?
6. If those answers are missing, you are paying for speed with no predictability.
7. We need workflow gates:
   - Intake
   - Qualification
   - Decision
   - PO trigger
8. Every catalog move should leave a reason trail.
9. The scorecard helps you map exactly where this breaks.

## Caption

Most teams don’t lose because their catalogs are bad. They lose because the process around them is weak.

Sourcing and review happen fast at first. Then everyone starts making local rules in notes, DMs, and different sheets.

The result is catalog debt.

If your supplier catalogs are not moving through a consistent workflow, your team is making faster wrong decisions.

Get the FBA Wholesale Ops Scorecard and identify your first workflow leak.

## CTA

Comment “SCORECARD” or DM “CATALOG” for the FBA Wholesale Ops Scorecard.

## DM Prompt

“Which step is weakest right now: supplier intake, qualification, decision logging, or handoff to buy-list?”

- Format: 1080 × 1350.
- Dark, command-center look with four stacked process cards.
- Slide 1-2: Problem framing.
- Slide 3-5: Checklist of missing owners/reasons.
- Slide 6-8: Workflow gate template.
- Slide 9: Scorecard CTA with link badge.
- Include supplier catalog callouts with clear status chips.
