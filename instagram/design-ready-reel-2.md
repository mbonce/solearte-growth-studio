# Instagram Asset Design Packet — Reel 2

## Final Title

The catalog-to-buy-list handoff is where chaos starts.

## Target Buyer

Amazon FBA wholesale sellers with VAs or sourcing teams managing supplier catalogs and buy-list decisions through spreadsheets.

## Content Pillar

Catalog analysis workflow + buy-list quality control.

## Pain Point

Catalogs get evaluated but buy-list decisions are made inconsistently, creating backlog and rework.

## Hook

The catalog-to-buy-list handoff is where chaos starts.

## Full Copy

**On-screen script:**

1. “Watch this handoff. One moment it’s a supplier catalog. Next moment it’s a purchase queue.”
2. “If there’s no qualification checklist, every team member applies their own bar.”
3. “That means the same catalog can be approved, dropped, and re-flagged by different people.”
4. “Then POs pile up, shipments drift, replenishment starts from memory.”
5. “Chaos isn’t in the catalog data.”
6. “Chaos is in the undefined handoff.”
7. “Define rules for: qualification, decision, and queueing.”
8. “Define who updates which field and why.”
9. “Then run the scorecard to see the first gap in your workflow.”

## Caption

Most FBA wholesale teams don’t lose margin from poor sourcing. They lose from handoff drift.

The catalog-to-buy-list switch is where decisions get interpreted, repeated, or skipped.

No one needs a perfect tool first.

They need a process where every handoff is visible and accountable.
