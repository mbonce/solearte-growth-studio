# Collab Outreach Sequences

Email or DM, three touches maximum, always specific to the partner.

## Touch 1

Subject: Color for [space/event/product]

"Hi [name], I'm SOLEARTé, a Denver contemporary artist working in saturated portraiture and wearable art. I've been in [space] / seen [thing] and thought about [specific idea: a wall, live painting on [date], a piece for [room]]. My work is about identity, resilience, and beginning again, and 15% of my Carry Your Colors collection supports RMIAN. If you're open to it, I'd love to send a short proposal. solearte.co"

## Touch 2 (5–7 days)

"Following up in case this got buried. One image attached of [relevant piece]. Happy to come by."

## Touch 3 (10–14 days)

"Last note from me. If timing is off, no problem, I'll be around Denver. Thanks for looking."
