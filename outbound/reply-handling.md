# Reply Handling

## Positive Reply

Thank them, confirm they are operating an FBA wholesale workflow, ask which area is messiest, and offer the scorecard or workflow audit.

## Not Interested

Keep it short. Acknowledge and close politely. Do not push.

## Beginner Seller

If they are not yet sourcing wholesale inventory, recommend waiting until they have active supplier outreach or catalogs to manage.

## Pricing Question

Say pricing is currently being validated through pilot-first conversations. Do not invent final SaaS pricing.

## Compliance/Account Health Question

Do not give account-health or legal advice. Frame Wholesale-Ops as documentation and workflow hygiene, not account issue prevention.

## Vendor Pitch

Decline politely unless the vendor directly supports the FBA wholesale workflow and has a clear reason for review.

## Angry Response

Apologize, do not argue, and stop outreach to that person.
