# Reply Handling

- **Yes / interested:** reply within 24 hours with the collab proposal template; log in `analytics/collab-pipeline.md`.
- **Budget question:** ask about scope first, then propose. Do not lead with a number.
- **Not now:** thank, ask if you can follow up in [n] months, log.
- **No:** thank, close, log the reason.
- **Wants free work:** see `sales/objection-handling.md`.
