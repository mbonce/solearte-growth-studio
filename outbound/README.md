# Outbound

Outbound should start practical conversations with Amazon FBA wholesale sellers who have operational complexity.

Use plain language. Do not pitch generic marketing services, agency work, distributor ERP, or guaranteed profit.

## Outbound Standard

Every outbound campaign needs:

- A clearly defined FBA wholesale segment.
- Public or approved evidence of fit.
- One workflow-specific reason for outreach.
- A low-friction offer: scorecard, workflow audit, or operator conversation.
- Reply handling rules before sending.

## Do Not

- Pretend to know private details.
- Use fake familiarity.
- Claim Wholesale-Ops can guarantee margin, supplier approval, or account safety.
- Send distributor ERP, retail, or warehouse-operator messaging.
