# Follow-Up Sequences

## After Scorecard Download

1. Ask which section scored lowest.
2. Send one practical cleanup suggestion.
3. Offer a workflow audit.

## After Positive Reply

1. Confirm they are an FBA wholesale seller.
2. Ask which workflow is most painful.
3. Offer scorecard or audit based on urgency.

## After Audit Interest

1. Confirm current tools.
2. Ask whether VAs or team members are involved.
3. Send booking link and set expectation: one workflow, practical review, no guaranteed outcomes.
