# Daily Prospecting Quota

Status: active no-contact prospecting system.

Goal: list at least 100 source-qualified potential customers per day.

Hard break: do not contact anyone. Do not send DMs, emails, comments, forms, connection requests, or supplier/prospect outreach.

## Definition Of A Listed Prospect

A row only counts toward the 100/day quota when it has:

- Public source URL.
- Prospect/account/company/person name or public source title.
- Source type.
- ICP fit signal.
- Workflow complexity signal.
- Score from 1-5.
- Status `Not contacted`.
- Next step `Research only`, `QA for fit`, or `Hold`.

Do not count invented names, unsourced rows, private profiles without public evidence, or generic search queries.

## Daily Source Mix

Target daily mix:

| Source Type | Daily Count | Why It Fits |
|---|---:|---|
| Public VA/job posts for Amazon FBA wholesale roles | 35 | Strong evidence of team workflow, catalog review, supplier outreach, spreadsheet/tool usage |
| LinkedIn public profiles/pages mentioning Amazon FBA wholesale operations | 25 | Founder/operator, seller, sourcing, or consultant signals |
| Instagram public accounts/posts in Amazon FBA wholesale lane | 20 | Active creator/seller ecosystem and audience discovery |
| Public forum/community threads with identifiable operators or businesses | 10 | Direct language around workflow pain |
| Podcasts/newsletters/tools/community pages with operator audiences | 10 | Partner/audience or prospect discovery |

Minimum score to prioritize: 3/5.

## Scoring

- 1 point: Amazon FBA wholesale signal.
- 1 point: supplier/catalog/price-list/ASIN-review signal.
- 1 point: VA/team/hiring/delegation signal.
- 1 point: spreadsheet/tool workflow signal.
- 1 point: repeat buying, PO, shipment, replenishment, or operations signal.

## Daily Workflow

1. Open or create `outbound/prospect-list-YYYY-MM-DD.md`.
2. Fill 100 source-qualified rows.
3. Do not contact anyone.
4. Mark disqualified leads with a reason.
5. Update `outbound/prospecting-daily-log.md`.
6. Add only the best 25 prospects to a future human-review outreach shortlist.

## Exclusions

- Pure retail arbitrage beginners.
- Private label-only sellers.
- Generic Amazon gurus with no wholesale operator signal.
- Traditional distributors, retail buyers, warehouse managers, or distributor ERP buyers.
- People seeking account-health guarantees, guaranteed ROI, or magic product-finding.
