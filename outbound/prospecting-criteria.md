# Prospecting Criteria

## Where To Find Prospects

- LinkedIn profiles mentioning Amazon wholesale, FBA wholesale, ecommerce operator, or wholesale seller.
- Public communities where FBA wholesale sellers discuss supplier outreach and catalog analysis.
- Podcasts, newsletters, and public posts about Amazon wholesale operations.
- Founder/operator profiles hiring VAs or sourcing team members.

## Signals Of Being An FBA Wholesale Seller

- Mentions Amazon FBA wholesale.
- Talks about supplier catalogs, brand approvals, or distributors.
- References Seller Central, FBA shipments, ASINs, buy lists, or replenishment.
- Discusses VAs for sourcing or Amazon operations.

## Signals Of Process Complexity

- Multiple suppliers.
- Catalog files or price lists.
- Team members or VAs.
- Repeat buying and replenishables.
- Manual PO, invoice, or shipment tracking.

## Signals They May Use VAs

- Hiring sourcing VAs.
- Talking about team review quality.
- Mentioning task delegation or SOPs.
- Public job posts for Amazon sourcing support.

## Signals Of Catalog Analysis Workflow

- Mentions analyzing catalogs.
- Uses Keepa, SmartScout, SellerAmp, or similar tools.
- Talks about ASIN matching or buy-list decisions.

## Exclusions

- Retail arbitrage beginners.
- Private label-only sellers.
- Traditional wholesale distributors.
- Brick-and-mortar retail buyers.
- Warehouse management or POS buyers.

## Prospect Scoring Model

- 1 point: public FBA wholesale signal.
- 1 point: supplier or catalog signal.
- 1 point: VA/team signal.
- 1 point: replenishment or repeat-buy signal.
- 1 point: public pain around spreadsheets or operations.

Prioritize prospects with 3 or more points.
