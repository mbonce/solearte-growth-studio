# Prospecting Criteria

Strong fit:

- Denver or Front Range spaces with walls and foot traffic (cafés, restaurants, co-working, boutiques, sneaker shops).
- Cultural organizations, immigrant-serving nonprofits, and community events.
- Brands with a genuine identity or resilience story that want art, not decoration.
- Event organizers wanting live painting or on-site portraits.
- Creators and accounts covering Denver art, diaspora stories, or sneaker culture.

Weak fit or disqualify:

- Anyone wanting the story without the artist involved.
- "Exposure only" offers with no audience or impact.
- Values that conflict with the RMIAN commitment.
