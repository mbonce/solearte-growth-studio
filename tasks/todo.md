## Investigation Summary

- This repo is a GTM operating shell for Wholesale-Ops, not an application codebase.
- The agency layer is the MarketingSkills submodule plus Wholesale-Ops-specific wrapper agents.
- The operating model is specialist-agent production with QA and human approval gates.
- Instagram is the primary launch channel.
- The FBA Wholesale Ops Scorecard is the primary CTA.
- Safe background work includes research synthesis, draft generation, QA review, asset queue updates, and daily reports.
- Unsafe autonomous work includes direct publishing, direct DMs/emails, pricing promises, account-health/compliance advice, fake results, unsupported product capabilities, and public customer claims.

## Proposed Agent Operating Model

- Orchestrator Agent: owns daily plan, task routing, QA queue, command center, and blocker reporting.
- FBA Product Marketing Agent: owns positioning, ICP fit, scorecard messaging, landing-page copy, objections, and approved wording.
- FBA Customer Research Agent: mines seller language, pain points, objections, and source-backed content ideas.
- Audience Research Agent: studies creator/content patterns, hooks, save/share/DM triggers, and competitive gaps.
- Instagram Content Agent: produces carousels, reels, stories, captions, DM prompts, and launch posts.
- FBA Ops Content Agent: repurposes approved Instagram ideas into X, LinkedIn, newsletter, and longer-form assets.
- Outbound Agent: drafts cold email, LinkedIn, and DM outreach batches for approval.
- Sales Enablement Agent: prepares audit scripts, demo scripts, one-pagers, proposals, and objection handling.
- Analytics Agent: records output volume, engagement, scorecard clicks, DMs, bookings, and learning loops.
- QA Agent: blocks, revises, or approves every public-facing asset before use.

## Proposed 24/7 Background Production Loop

- Every 4 hours: Orchestrator checks queue, assigns next draft tasks, and records blockers.
- Overnight: Research, VOC mining, hook generation, carousel/reel/story drafting, repurposing, and QA pre-checks.
- Morning: Daily digest of assets created, QA status, blockers, and human decisions needed.
- Afternoon: Revision pass on QA feedback and preparation of next-day publish-ready packets.
- Evening: Analytics and learning loop update; next overnight queue seeded.
