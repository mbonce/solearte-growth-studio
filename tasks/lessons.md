# Lessons

- 2026-06-12: When generating marketing graphics in this repo, do not default to polished corporate dashboard boards. Use a stronger graphic-design language with oversized type, asymmetry, angle, contrast, and poster-style composition while preserving the internal-only and fake-row constraints.
- 2026-06-12: After Mike asked for graphics to look more like `@legacyxfba`, do not default to abstract editorial posters. Favor creator-style FBA growth graphics with darker social-native backgrounds, louder hook-first headlines, tighter card stacks, more obvious hierarchy, and stronger “Instagram business post” framing while preserving internal-only, fake-row, and founder-gated constraints.
- 2026-06-16: When comparing graphics tools, do not normalize every tool into the same visual template. The comparison needs high visual variety across tools and briefs, plain Amazon marketplace language, no unexplained jargon like ASIN, and no slash-heavy footer copy.
