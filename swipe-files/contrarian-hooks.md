# Contrarian Hook Swipe File

## Anti-Guru Hooks

- More products will not fix a broken operation.
- A product research tool will not manage your buy-list handoff.
- Your VA is not the operating system.
- Sourcing volume can make the business harder to control.
- Passive-income language does not belong in FBA wholesale operations.

## Operator Reframes

- The problem is not the spreadsheet. The problem is no workflow.
- The goal is not more tabs. It is clearer decisions.
- The second buy needs more discipline than the first.
- The founder's memory is not a system.
- Delegation without review states is not leverage.
