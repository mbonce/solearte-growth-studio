# Operational Hooks

## Supplier Hooks

- Supplier follow-up needs status, owner, and next action.
- A supplier list is not a pipeline.
- Approval status should not live in Gmail.

## Catalog Hooks

- Catalog review needs decision states.
- Matched ASINs are not approved buys.
- Rejection reasons save future rework.

## Buy-List Hooks

- A buy list needs approval logic.
- Every buy-list item should explain why now.
- Buy-list chaos usually starts upstream.

## VA Hooks

- Your VA needs criteria, not guesses.
- Review states make delegation auditable.
- Hidden VA notes create founder bottlenecks.

## Replenishment Hooks

- Replenishment from memory is not a system.
- Reorder decisions need updated assumptions.
- Margin drift hides before the second buy.
