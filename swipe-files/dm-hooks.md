# DM Hook Swipe File

## Qualification Prompts

- Are you currently doing Amazon FBA wholesale?
- Are you reviewing supplier catalogs or price lists?
- Do VAs or sourcing team members help with review?
- Which workflow is messiest: suppliers, catalogs, buy lists, shipments, or replenishment?
- Are replenishables part of your business?

## Scorecard Delivery Prompts

- I can send the scorecard. Which workflow feels weakest right now?
- The scorecard is built for spreadsheet-heavy FBA wholesale workflows.
- If you have active catalogs or buy lists, it should be useful.

## Human Review Prompts

- Pricing is still being validated. I can route that for human follow-up.
- Account-health specifics need human review.
- Demo requests work best after a quick workflow audit.
