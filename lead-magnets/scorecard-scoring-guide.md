# Scorecard Scoring Guide

Use this guide after total score calculation.

Scoring uses 10 sections at 0–5 each (total 0–50).

- 0–20: workflow is mostly reactive
- 21–35: workflow works but is fragile
- 36–45: workflow is structured but inconsistent
- 46–50: workflow is mature and reviewable

## 0–20: Mostly Reactive

### What it means

- Teams are using memory, notes, or fragmented channels for core operations.
- Decisions are often repeated or delayed.

### Likely symptoms

- Missing supplier follow-ups
- Catalog reviews not completed on schedule
- Buy-list decisions made without context
- VA blockers hidden until late in replenishment

### Recommended next step

- Start with one section and apply a one-week process cleanup.
- Prioritize supplier pipeline + catalog intake first.

### Content to send

- Intro scorecard follow-up email
- "Where to start first" checklist
- Offer for 30-minute workflow audit

## 21–35: Workflow Works but is Fragile

### What it means

- Core process exists but depends heavily on specific people and spreadsheets.
- Execution breaks under sourcing volume or staffing changes.

### Likely symptoms

- Inconsistent ASIN criteria
- POs not tied to approved buy-list decisions
- Replenishment run by memory

### Recommended next step

- Convert one painful handoff into a documented workflow.
- Add review gates between catalog intake, ASIN review, and buy list.

### Content to send

- Workflow map template for the lowest section
- Supplier/campaign follow-up sequence
- Pilot framing centered on repeatable control points

## 36–45: Structured but Inconsistent

### What it means

- Good operating foundation is present, but quality slips and team alignment is uneven.
- Most tasks are tracked, but edge cases are unresolved.

### Likely symptoms

- Some decision history missing
- Margin checks happen late
- Reconciliation varies by team member

### Recommended next step

- Add a second-level review cadence (weekly).
- Target the lowest section with the highest operational risk.

### Content to send

- Advanced workflow checklist
- KPI definitions for team visibility
- Offer an ops workflow audit to harden handoffs

## 46–50: Mature and Reviewable

### What it means

- Operations are mostly reviewable and role-based.
- Team can hand over work with fewer founder interventions.

### Likely symptoms

- Infrequent but costly edge-case failures
- Low-volume blind spots in replenishment or risk control

### Recommended next step

- Keep a monthly scorecard rhythm.
- Apply improvements to the highest impact risk area only.

### Content to send

- "Mature operator" maintenance checklist
- Upgrade focus on consistency and exception handling
- Case-based workflow audit invitation if they are adding scale
