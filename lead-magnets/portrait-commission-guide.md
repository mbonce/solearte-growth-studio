# Portrait Commission Guide (PDF or site page)

**Title:** How a portrait commission works with SOLEARTé

1. **Tell me who it's for.** A parent, a partner, a child, an elder, yourself, a pet. And the occasion.
2. **Tell me where it will live.** Size and color depend on the room and the light.
3. **Send a few photos.** Imperfect is fine. I paint what the photo can't show.
4. **I reply with scope, price, and timing.** Nothing begins until you say yes.
5. **I paint.** You'll see progress if you want to.
6. **Delivery.** Denver pickup or shipping, confirmed as part of scope.

**What my portraits are:** saturated, expressive, alive. Not photo-realistic.

**Start:** solearté@gmail.com or DM "portrait" on Instagram.
