# Follow-Up Sequence

After someone asks for the guide or answers the planner:

- Day 0: send the guide; thank them; ask one question about the subject.
- Day 3: if no reply, share one finished portrait with its story. No pitch.
- Day 10: "Still here whenever you're ready. Commissions are open through [month]."
- Then stop. Add to the newsletter list only with permission.
