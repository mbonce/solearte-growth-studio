# Commission Planner Questions

Send as a story, a DM, or a one-page PDF.

1. Who do you want to see on your wall?
2. What's the occasion, or is there one?
3. Where will it hang, and how much light is there?
4. What colors do you love? What colors do you avoid?
5. When does it need to be done?

"Answer these and send them to me. That's the whole first step."
