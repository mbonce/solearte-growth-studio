# Scorecard to Workflow Audit Handoff

## Offer Triggers

- Low score (0–20 total)
- Mentions VA or team coordination gaps
- Mentions catalog backlog or review delays
- Mentions buy-list confusion
- Mentions replenishment from memory
- Asks about product outcomes without process context
- Asks for demo too early
- Describes messy workflow with no repeatable owner and status

## Handoff Rules

1. Confirm scorecard completion and lowest section.
2. Ask three qualifying questions:
   - Which section hurts the team the most this week?
   - Who is blocked in the flow?
   - What does a fix look like in one week?
3. Route to audit scheduling only if they answer with a workflow-specific bottleneck.

## Offer Structure

- Primary: 30-Day FBA Wholesale Ops Cleanup Pilot
- Secondary: 60-minute workflow audit before scope lock
- Human approval required for claims, pricing, or sensitive account-health requests

## Non-Handoff Cases

- Too early stage / no repeat buy-list process.
- No current team coordination or supplier volume.
- Pure tool migration request without workflow problem context.

## Sample Handoff Prompt

“The scorecard shows your highest risk in {{lowest_section}}. Let’s run a focused workflow audit on that section first and turn it into a repeatable operating map.”
