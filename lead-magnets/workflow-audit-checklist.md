# Workflow Audit Checklist

## Before The Audit

- Confirm the seller is an Amazon FBA wholesale seller.
- Ask what tools they use today.
- Identify whether VAs or sourcing team members are involved.
- Ask which workflow feels messiest.

## Audit Areas

- Supplier pipeline.
- Catalog intake.
- ASIN review.
- Buy-list approval.
- Margin assumptions.
- PO and invoice tracking.
- FBA shipment workflow.
- Replenishment decisions.
- VA task ownership.
- Risk-control notes.

## Evidence To Request

- Example supplier tracker.
- Example catalog review sheet.
- Example buy list.
- Example PO or invoice workflow.
- Example replenishment decision.

## Output

- Workflow map.
- Bottlenecks.
- Risky gaps.
- Quick cleanup recommendations.
- Pilot fit recommendation.
