# Launch Command Center

**Goal:** by end of week one, the Instagram account tells the three stories (portraits, Carry Your Colors, commissions open), every DM is answered, and the site has no broken facts.

**Three numbers to watch (record, don't chase):** commission inquiries, Carry Your Colors orders, shares of the collection story.

**Daily:** post per calendar, reply first hour, triage inbox twice, log inquiries.
**Weekly:** report, learn, adjust.

**Where things live:** content in `instagram/week-1-content-queue.md`, approvals in `launch/approval-board.md`, risks in `launch/risk-register.md`.
