# Launch Command Center

## 1. Launch Objective

Launch Wholesale-Ops Instagram-first and drive qualified FBA wholesale sellers to the FBA Wholesale Ops Scorecard, then route high-fit conversations toward ops workflow audits.

## 2. Target ICP

Amazon FBA wholesale sellers with VAs or sourcing teams who are managing supplier catalogs and buy-list decisions through spreadsheets.

## 3. Primary Channel

Instagram.

## 4. Primary CTA

Get the FBA Wholesale Ops Scorecard.

## 5. Secondary CTA

Book an ops workflow audit.

## 10. Open Blockers

- Instagram handle not confirmed.
- Link in bio not confirmed.
- Booking link not confirmed.
- Scorecard page not manually reviewed.
- First five posts not founder-approved.

## 11. Human Decisions Needed

- Final Instagram handle.
- Final bio.
- Link in bio destination.
- Booking link destination.
- Approval of DM replies for pricing, account health, and demo requests.
- Approval to publish first five posts.

## 12. Daily Operating Rhythm

`[Morning QA] --> [Publish] --> [Monitor DMs] --> [Log Metrics] --> [Review Blockers]`

Morning:
- Check approval board.
- Confirm today's post and stories.
- Confirm scorecard link.

Midday:
- Monitor comments and DMs.
- Route risky replies to human approval.
- Capture seller language.

Evening:
- Record metrics.
- Review blockers.
- Update tomorrow's publishing status.
