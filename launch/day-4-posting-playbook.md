# Day 4 Posting Playbook

## Day 4 Playbook (Instagram)

**Asset:** Reel 2 — The catalog-to-buy-list handoff is where chaos starts.

## 1) Objective

Expose catalog-to-buy-list breakdown risk and increase qualified scorecard conversations from operators with real workflow gaps.

## 2) Target Audience

Amazon FBA wholesale sellers with VAs or sourcing teams managing supplier catalogs and buy-list decisions through spreadsheets.

## 3) Post timing placeholder

- Preferred window: Week-1 Day 4, late morning local time (placeholder)
- Alternative window: 1-hour later if high founder-review load

## 4) Reel asset checklist

- [ ] `design-ready-reel-2.md` final version approved.
- [ ] Export ready and subtitle file aligned.
- [ ] Story sequence prepared: VA workflow story.
- [ ] DM scripts loaded with escalation logic.
- [ ] Caption CTA verified.

## 5) Caption

`The catalog-to-buy-list handoff is where chaos starts.`

`One catalog can move through three people and three opinions before a single buy-list decision.`

`Comment “scorecard” or DM “BUY LIST” for the FBA Wholesale Ops Scorecard.`

## 6) CTA

Primary CTA: `Comment “scorecard” or DM “BUY LIST” for the FBA Wholesale Ops Scorecard.`

Secondary CTA: `After you run it, send your lowest-scoring section: suppliers, catalogs, buy list, shipments, replenishment, VA workflow, or risk control.`

## 7) Story sequence after posting

- Sequence: VA workflow story
- First frame: “Who owns review handoff?”
- Poll: owner in team, owner in VA, owner in seller.
- Core frame: two-slide workflow example with clear decision and trigger.
- Final frame: scorecard CTA.

## 8) First-hour engagement checklist

- Prioritize comments describing handoff pain.
- Post one anchor response that invites a concrete example.
- Keep first-hour replies short and diagnostic.
- Capture one “best hook phrase” candidates from incoming comments.

## 9) DM handling rules

- Scorecard request: send `{{scorecard_link}}`, then one-stage diagnostic question.
- VA process questions: ask if owner and handoff criteria are documented.
- Qualified operator signals: send targeted next-step question around buy-list transition.
- High-risk topics: escalate to founder (pricing/account-health/demo/integration).

## 10) Comment handling rules

- Tag and prioritize “I’m seeing this” and “This is me” comments.
- Avoid abstract motivational replies.
- Keep focus on the operational system, not the creator personality.

## 11) Metrics to capture

- likes
- shares
- comments
- profile visits
- link clicks
- DMs
- scorecard requests

## 12) End-of-day review checklist

- [ ] Count of handoff-related DMs.
- [ ] First-hour scorecard request conversion.
- [ ] Best-performing reel segment/scene.
- [ ] Any friction in link delivery.
- [ ] Update `analytics/day-4-metrics-log.md`.

## 13) What signals indicate strong positioning resonance

- Users explicitly mention handoff ownership or duplicate decision loops.
- Buyers ask what to do with buy-list confusion next.
- Scorecard request rate lifts vs Day 3.

## 14) Operational note

If comments become repetitive, move the remaining reply volume into the same-thread question loop and hold for tomorrow’s Q&A comments.
