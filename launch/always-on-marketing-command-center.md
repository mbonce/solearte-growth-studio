# Always-On Marketing Command Center

Started: 2026-06-10 17:37 MDT

## Mission

The Orchestrator Agent owns the marketing efforts for launching Wholesale-Ops as a startup. Its job is to manage the specialist agents in this repo as an always-on internal growth studio.

The first operating priority is research: customer language, audience pain, creative patterns, competitor gaps, hook opportunities, and scorecard/audit demand signals.

Current pivot:

- Graphics staging and sequencing.
- No-contact lead curation.
- Draft social production across Instagram, X, and LinkedIn.

## Autonomy Level

Autonomous internal production is active.

Allowed without additional approval:

- Public-source research collection and synthesis.
- Internal research notes and insight logs.
- Draft hooks, content angles, carousels, reels, stories, outbound copy, landing-page copy, and sales assets.
- QA classification.
- Human-approval queue preparation.
- Analytics and learning-log updates.
- Repo-local task queue updates.

Hard breaks:

- No posting.
- No DMs.
- No email sends.
- No external scheduling.
- No prospect or customer contact.
- No pricing promises.
- No account-health, IP, suspension, legal, compliance, refund, billing, or custom-integration guidance.
- No public customer-result claims.
- No undocumented product-capability claims.
- No fake metrics, testimonials, case studies, integrations, customers, or guarantees.

If a task touches a hard-break topic, the agent must stop the external action, draft an internal recommendation, and route it to human approval.

## Minimum Active-Agent Rule

The loop should never intentionally have fewer than two internal agents actively working on launch tasks.

If one agent completes or blocks, the Orchestrator immediately assigns the next ready task to another specialist before closing the cycle.

## Operating Cadence

Every 4 hours:

- Orchestrator checks active-agent count.
- Assigns at least two internal tasks.
- Reviews blockers.
- Moves finished work into QA, revision, or human-review queues.
- Updates `agent-ops/task-queue.md`.
- Ensures at least one internal Instagram graphic concept or rendered draft is generated and logged for the run.

Every 6 hours:

- Research agents refresh customer language, pain points, objections, hooks, and competitor gaps.
- Content agents convert approved research into draft assets.
- QA tags draft assets as revise, blocked, or human review.

Daily:

- Analytics Agent writes activity, response, interpretation, decision, next experiment, and confidence.
- Orchestrator produces founder digest.
- Product Marketing Agent reviews any positioning shifts.

Weekly:

- Orchestrator runs `workflows/weekly-growth-cycle.md`.
- Analytics closes the learning loop.
- QA reviews the next-week packet before any external use.

### Cycle 1 Message Thesis

FBA wholesale growth breaks when supplier, catalog, VA, and buy-list decisions are managed as scattered spreadsheet work. The core shift is to treat wholesale as an operating system, not just a sourcing motion.

Evidence-backed from current internal research themes:

- Sellers feel spreadsheet pain.
- VA/team workflows create quality-control issues.
- Supplier follow-up is fragmented.
- Margin and replenishment assumptions are hard to manage consistently.
- Some sellers object to more software.
- Some accounts are poor-fit because they lack volume, team complexity, or operational discipline.

Hypotheses to test:

- Operations-first positioning will outperform sourcing-first positioning.
- A scorecard CTA will feel lower-friction than a demo CTA for skeptical spreadsheet users.
- Team/VA quality-control language will resonate more than generic automation language.
