# Day 5 Posting Playbook

## Day 5 Playbook (Instagram)

**Asset:** Carousel 3 — Hiring a VA Does Not Fix a Broken FBA Wholesale Process

## 1) Objective

Close Week 1 with a founder/operator-facing execution message and push serious conversations into scorecard + audit path.

## 2) Target Audience

Amazon FBA wholesale sellers with VAs or sourcing teams managing supplier catalogs and buy-list decisions through spreadsheets.

## 3) Post timing placeholder

- Preferred window: Week-1 Day 5, late morning local time (placeholder)
- Alternative window: 1-hour later if weekend handoff planning is in progress

## 4) Carousel asset checklist

- [ ] `design-ready-carousel-3.md` approved and exported.
- [ ] Final caption includes no high-risk claims.
- [ ] Story sequence prepared: replenishment memory story.
- [ ] Comment responses prepared for “This does not fix my problems” type.
- [ ] DM escalation paths reviewed.

## 5) Caption

`A VA can move tasks faster, but only if your workflow is reviewable.`

`If ownership, decision rationale, and replenishment context are not documented, teams confuse speed with progress.`

`Comment “VA” or DM “WORKFLOW” for the FBA Wholesale Ops Scorecard.`

## 6) CTA

Primary CTA: `Comment “VA” or DM “WORKFLOW” for the FBA Wholesale Ops Scorecard.`

Secondary CTA: `Tell me where the handoff breaks first: catalog, buy list, shipments, replenishment, or VA task handoff.`

## 7) Story sequence after posting

- Sequence: Replenishment story
- First frame: “Do you reorder from a system or from memory?”
- Poll: system / memory / partial.
- Core frame: where replenishment decisions get made.
- Final frame: scorecard CTA + book-audit invitation note.

## 8) First-hour engagement checklist

- Respond quickly to operational comments and scorecard asks.
- Keep replies practical and short.
- Pull 3 strong pain phrases for audit handoff qualification.
- Flag if replenishment appears to be top pain area.

## 9) DM handling rules

- “Can this help with replenishment?”: frame as workflow scorecard + replenishment section.
- “Can I see a demo?”: escalate to founder.
- “Does this guarantee…”: safe answer + founder escalation note.
- “What should I fix first?”: scorecard-first and lowest-section question.

## 10) Comment handling rules

- Prioritize comments with concrete replenishment or VA handoff problems.
- Use “what breaks first in your week?” as an entry question.
- Avoid over-discussion on tool names and integrations.

## 11) Metrics to capture

- likes
- saves
- shares
- comments
- profile visits
- link clicks
- DMs
- scorecard requests

## 12) End-of-day review checklist

- [ ] Review scorecard request quality by segment.
- [ ] Record any founder escalation pattern (rate and reason).
- [ ] Determine if replenishment is Week 2 leading topic.
- [ ] Update `analytics/day-5-metrics-log.md`.
- [ ] Prepare Week 1 learning summary for Monday planning.

## 13) What signals indicate strong positioning resonance

- Operators mention team workflow, not only tools.
- Direct references to recurring missed handoffs and unclear ownership.
- More than one seller asks for workflow audit after scorecard.

## 14) Operational note

End Day 5 by deciding whether we keep same thesis cadence next week or pivot to replenishment/VA-heavy variants based on top pain points.
