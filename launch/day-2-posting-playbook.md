# Day 2 Posting Playbook

## Day 2 Playbook (Instagram)

**Asset:** Reel 1 — FBA wholesale is not just sourcing. It is operations.

## 1) Objective

Reinforce the positioning shift: sourcing capability without operating discipline is fragile, and get sellers to request scorecard diagnostics.

## 2) Target Audience

Amazon FBA wholesale sellers with VAs or sourcing teams managing supplier catalogs and buy-list decisions through spreadsheets.

## 3) Post timing placeholder

- Preferred window: Week-1 Day 2, late morning local time (placeholder)
- Alternative window: 1-hour later if Day 1 response load is high

## 4) Reel asset checklist

- [ ] `design-ready-reel-1.md` final script approved.
- [ ] Export file ready (1080 × 1920).
- [ ] Caption includes scorecard-first CTA.
- [ ] Story sequence prepared: Catalog backlog.
- [ ] B-roll order and on-screen text verified.
- [ ] Low-risk DM script loaded.

## 5) Caption

`More sourcing volume makes handoffs louder, not safer.`

`If nobody owns supplier follow-up, catalog decisions, and buy-list transitions, teams get faster at the wrong thing.`

`Comment “scorecard” or DM “OPS” for the FBA Wholesale Ops Scorecard.`

## 6) CTA

Primary CTA: `Comment “scorecard” or DM “OPS” for the FBA Wholesale Ops Scorecard.`

Secondary CTA: `After you run it, send me your lowest-scoring section: suppliers, catalogs, buy list, shipments, replenishment, VA workflow, or risk control.`

## 7) Story sequence after posting

- Sequence: Catalog backlog story
- First frame: “How many supplier catalogs are waiting for review?”
- Poll: “0–5 / 6–15 / 15+ waiting.”
- Core frame: checklist of catalog review gates (intake, qualification, approval, PO).
- Final frame: DM OPS for scorecard.

## 8) First-hour engagement checklist

- Pin one relevant comment that mirrors the key reel thesis.
- Answer first 5 comments manually with concise operational framing.
- Capture first 3 recurring objections or confusion points.
- Do not over-reply. Let comments breathe.

## 9) DM handling rules

- Scorecard requests: send link first, then ask qualifying section.
- “What is this?”: short explanation, no over-qualification.
- Workflow pain comments: ask for one concrete example.
- Pricing/account-health/demo/integration: founder escalation.

## 10) Comment handling rules

- Encourage specific operator language by replying:
  - “Which step breaks first in your handoff?”
- Avoid broad debates.
- Redirect generic product questions to `instagram/dm-response-library.md` safe responses.

## 11) Metrics to capture

- likes
- shares
- comments
- profile visits
- link clicks
- DMs
- scorecard requests

## 12) End-of-day review checklist

- [ ] Did reel produce scorecard asks in first hour?
- [ ] Which line triggered most saves/shares?
- [ ] Were catalog-related questions clearer than Day 1?
- [ ] Which objections need a follow-up post topic?
- [ ] Update `analytics/day-2-metrics-log.md`.

## 13) What signals indicate strong positioning resonance

- Comments mention “handoff,” “qualification,” “decision criteria,” or “owner.”
- Sellers ask what to fix before adding more inventory.
- Direct requests for scorecard within 1–2 hours.

## 14) Operational note

Keep this reel practical, not inspirational. Repost as a story cut only if needed to test hook strength.
