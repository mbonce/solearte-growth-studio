# Day 1 Posting Playbook

## Day 1 Playbook (Instagram)

**Asset:** Carousel 1 — Your FBA Wholesale Spreadsheet Is Becoming Technical Debt

## 1) Objective

Launch the Week 1 sequence with a practical operations-first anchor post and move qualified sellers into the scorecard flow.

## 2) Target Audience

Amazon FBA wholesale sellers with VAs or sourcing teams who are managing supplier catalogs and buy-list decisions through spreadsheets.

## 3) Post timing placeholder

- Preferred window: Week-1 Day 1, late morning local time (placeholder)
- Alternative window: 1-hour later if founder is unavailable

## 4) Carousel asset checklist

- [ ] `design-ready-carousel-1.md` final version approved.
- [ ] Export assets ready (`carousel1-slide01.png` → `carousel1-slide09.png`).
- [ ] Captions copied exactly with scorecard CTA.
- [ ] Link placeholder set to `{{scorecard_link}}` in final slide/notes.
- [ ] Story sequence ready to post immediately after.
- [ ] Comment/DM low-risk responses ready.

## 5) Caption

`Your FBA wholesale spreadsheet is becoming technical debt.`

`This is not a tooling problem. It is usually missing workflow control for supplier, catalog, and buy-list handoffs.`

`Comment “scorecard” or DM “OPS” for the FBA Wholesale Ops Scorecard.`

## 6) CTA

Primary CTA: `Comment “scorecard” or DM “OPS” for the FBA Wholesale Ops Scorecard.`

Secondary CTA: `After you run it, send your lowest-scoring section: suppliers, catalogs, buy list, shipments, replenishment, VA workflow, or risk control.`

## 7) Story sequence after posting

- Sequence: Supplier follow-up story
- First frame: “Where do supplier follow-ups currently live?”
- Sticker: poll (Where do follow-ups live? spreadsheet, notes, emails, mixed)
- Third frame: show “single owner, single status, single next action” rule.
- Final frame: “DM OPS for the scorecard”

## 8) First-hour engagement checklist

- Approve first 10 comments within 30 minutes.
- Reply using preloaded templates in `instagram/comment-reply-library.md`.
- Post scorecard link first for all scorecard requests.
- Ask one follow-up diagnostic question only if user asked for deeper next step.
- Tag possible candidates with buyer phrase note.

## 9) DM handling rules

- Scorecard request: send link first (`{{scorecard_link}}`), then qualifying question.
- Beginner sellers: invite to describe current workflow, then offer focused starter advice.
- Pricing/account-health/demo/integration/profit questions: route to founder immediately.
- Keep every DM tone human, short, and operational.

## 10) Comment handling rules

- Prioritize comments showing workflow complexity.
- Prioritize real Amazon FBA operator indicators.
- Reply with one practical question if comment is qualified:
  - “Which part is least visible today: suppliers, catalogs, buy list, shipments, replenishment, VA workflow, or risk control?”
- Keep no-hype, no guarantees.

## 11) Metrics to capture

- likes
- saves
- shares
- comments
- profile visits
- link clicks
- DMs
- scorecard requests

## 12) End-of-day review checklist

- [ ] Number of scorecard requests.
- [ ] Which post frame or line got strongest comment follow-up.
- [ ] Any high-risk replies handled by founder.
- [ ] Any repeated phrase that repeats across comments.
- [ ] Update `analytics/day-1-metrics-log.md` and Week 1 plan based on signals.

## 13) What signals indicate strong positioning resonance

- Sellers mention “can’t get from catalog to buy list fast enough”.
- Comments use process terms: ownership, review state, next action.
- “This is exactly where I am” or “This is me” appears with details.
- Immediate scorecard ask within first hour.

## 14) Operational note

If launch momentum feels low, do not switch tone. Repost the same post after 24 hours with minor copy reinforcement and keep first-hour response discipline.
