## What To Capture

- Hook text.
- Format: reel, carousel, story, meme, live, screenshot.
- Repeated comments.
- CTAs used.
- Visual style.
- Whether operations after sourcing are addressed.

## Opportunity

Most Instagram creator content appears likely to cluster around sourcing, beginner education, tools, and motivation. Wholesale-Ops should test a sharper lane: operating discipline for sellers whose catalogs, VAs, buy lists, and replenishment decisions are already messy.
