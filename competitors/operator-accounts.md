# Operator Accounts

This file tracks accounts outside pure FBA that model strong operator content: systems, workflow, SOPs, dashboards, ops discipline, and founder leverage.

## Account Types

- Ecommerce operators.
- Agency operators.
- Ops consultants.
- Systems builders.
- Notion/Airtable workflow creators.
- Founder productivity accounts.

## Patterns To Borrow

- Before/after workflow breakdowns.
- "Here is the system" carousels.
- Command-center visuals.
- Checklist lead magnets.
- Daily operating cadence posts.

## Patterns To Avoid

- Generic productivity content.
- Founder lifestyle content.
- Over-polished SaaS visuals.
- Automation claims that do not match Wholesale-Ops.

## Wholesale-Ops Adaptation

Translate broad operator content into FBA wholesale specifics: supplier status, catalog review, ASIN decisions, buy lists, POs, FBA shipments, replenishment, VA accountability, and risk-control hygiene.
