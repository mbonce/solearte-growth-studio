# Carousel Patterns

| Pattern | Slides | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| How a commission works | 6–8 | Answer "how much / how long / what do I send" without a price |
| One piece, many angles | 5–8 | Sneakers (site already has eight views) |
| Before / layers / after | 4–6 | Process trust |
| The story behind the collection | 5–7 | Carry Your Colors, ends on the RMIAN line |
| Color study | 4–5 | Palette, why these colors, saves |
| Available work | 3–6 | Collector inquiries |

## Design

- Slide 1 is a finished piece or a face, never text-only.
- Big Poppins-style bold headline, few words.
- Final slide: one CTA and the handle.
