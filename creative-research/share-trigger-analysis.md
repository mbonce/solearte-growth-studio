# Share Trigger Analysis

## What Gets Shared

- Content that makes operators feel seen.
- Posts that call out spreadsheet chaos without shaming.
- VA workflow posts that founders want team members to see.
- Contrarian anti-guru takes.
- Simple workflow diagrams.

## Why They Share

- The post explains their frustration better than they can.
- It can align the team around a process problem.
- It challenges over-focus on sourcing.
- It creates a useful internal conversation.

## Share-Oriented Angles

- "Your VA should not be the operating system."
- "More sourcing volume will expose weak operations."
- "A buy list without decision history is just another spreadsheet."

## Guardrail

Do not write blame-based content. Make the process the problem, not the person.
