# Save Trigger Analysis

## What FBA Wholesale Operators Save

- Catalog review checklists.
- Buy-list approval criteria.
- VA review-state diagrams.
- Margin review prompts.
- Replenishment decision checklists.
- Supplier pipeline field lists.

## Why They Save

- They want a reusable process.
- They want to send it to a VA or teammate.
- It names a problem they know but have not systematized.
- It creates a checklist for the next catalog, buy list, or reorder.

## Creative Formula

`[Recognizable Pain] --> [Operating Principle] --> [Checklist] --> [Scorecard CTA]`

## Test Ideas

- "Save this before your next supplier catalog."
- "Use this before moving an ASIN to buy list."
- "Send this to the VA reviewing your next catalog."
