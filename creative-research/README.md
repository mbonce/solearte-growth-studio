# Creative Research

This folder captures content structures that can turn FBA wholesale operational pain into Instagram-native creative.

Use it to improve:

- Hooks.
- Carousels.
- Reels.
- Stories.
- Visual direction.
- Save/share/DM triggers.
- Landing-page angles.
- Lead magnet framing.

## Creative Research Rule

Do not copy creators. Extract patterns: hook type, visual structure, emotional driver, audience reaction, CTA, and reusable lesson.
