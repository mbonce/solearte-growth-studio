# DM Trigger Analysis

## What Triggers Useful DMs

- Scorecard offers.
- "Which workflow is messiest?" prompts.
- Specific bottleneck labels: catalog backlog, buy-list confusion, VA handoff, replenishment from memory.
- Posts that ask for the lowest-scoring workflow.

## Good DM Prompts

- "DM OPS for the scorecard."
- "Reply with your messiest workflow."
- "DM CATALOG if your catalog-to-buy-list handoff is messy."
- "DM VA if your VA review process is hard to audit."

## Qualification Signals

- Mentions supplier catalogs.
- Mentions VAs or sourcing teams.
- Mentions buy lists.
- Mentions replenishment.
- Mentions spreadsheet chaos.

## Escalation Signals

- Pricing details.
- Amazon account health.
- IP complaints.
- Suspensions.
- Legal/compliance questions.
- Profit guarantee questions.
