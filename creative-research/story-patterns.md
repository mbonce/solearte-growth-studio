# Story Patterns

- Morning studio: what is on the easel today.
- Poll: "Which color goes here?" (engagement, low effort).
- Question box: "What would you commission?" (commission leads).
- Behind a Carry Your Colors order being packed (with the RMIAN line).
- Reposting followers wearing the sneakers (with permission).
- Countdown or reminder only for real events (markets, shows, live painting).

Highlights (see `instagram/highlights-plan.md`): Commissions, Carry Your Colors, Process, Available, About.
