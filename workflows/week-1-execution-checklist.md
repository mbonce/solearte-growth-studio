# Week 1 Instagram Execution Checklist

## Campaign

- Theme: FBA wholesale is not just sourcing. It is operations.
- Target: Amazon FBA wholesale sellers with VAs or sourcing teams managing supplier catalogs and buy-list decisions through spreadsheets.
- Primary CTA: Get the FBA Wholesale Ops Scorecard.

## Daily Posting Plan

### Monday

- Publish Carousel 1: Your FBA Wholesale Spreadsheet Is Becoming Technical Debt.
- Stories: supplier follow-up chaos.
- Engagement: reply to comments and DMs for 20 minutes after posting.

### Tuesday

- Publish Reel 1: FBA wholesale is not just sourcing.
- Stories: catalog review backlog.
- Engagement: ask qualified commenters which workflow is messiest.

### Wednesday

- Publish Carousel 2: A Supplier Catalog Is Not an Opportunity Until It Has Workflow.
- Stories: buy-list approval confusion.
- Engagement: send scorecard to qualified "CATALOG" or "OPS" DMs.

### Thursday

- Publish Reel 2: The catalog-to-buy-list handoff is where chaos starts.
- Stories: VA workflow gaps.
- Engagement: route demo, pricing, and account-health questions to human review.

### Friday

- Publish Carousel 3: Hiring a VA Does Not Fix a Broken FBA Wholesale Process.
- Stories: replenishment from memory.
- Engagement: collect repeated pain phrases and update research notes.

### Weekend

- Publish one meme angle if a Week 1 pain pattern is obvious.
- Repost the strongest story poll result.
- Review metrics and choose next week's workflow theme.
