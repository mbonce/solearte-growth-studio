# Research Loop

This workflow keeps audience and creative research moving every week.

## Monday: Research Creators

- Review Instagram, X, and YouTube creators in the FBA wholesale and Amazon seller ecosystem.
- Capture hook patterns, format patterns, visual patterns, and positioning gaps.
- Update `competitors/` and `creative-research/`.

## Tuesday: Research Audience Comments

- Review public comments on relevant creator posts and Wholesale-Ops content.
- Capture exact seller language.
- Separate beginner questions from scaling-operator pain.
- Update `voice-of-customer/` and `audience-research/top-pain-points.md`.

## Wednesday: Research DM Patterns

- Review scorecard requests, workflow pain DMs, objections, and qualification replies.
- Identify DM triggers and escalation patterns.
- Update `creative-research/dm-trigger-analysis.md` and `voice-of-customer/buying-language.md`.

## Thursday: Research Competitor Positioning

- Review creator and tool positioning.
- Identify gaps around operations, workflow maturity, VA accountability, replenishment, and founder psychology.
- Update `competitors/positioning-gaps.md`.

## Friday: Update Hooks, Swipe Files, And Content Opportunities

- Add new hooks to `creative-research/high-performing-hooks.md`.
- Add reusable structures to `swipe-files/`.
- Add insights to `analytics/research-insights-log.md`.
- Feed the best opportunities to Instagram, outbound, sales, website, and product positioning.

## Weekly Output

- 3 new audience insights.
- 5 new hooks or hook variants.
- 3 voice-of-customer phrases.
- 1 positioning gap.
- 1 next-week Instagram content opportunity.
