# Instagram Inbox Management

## Permission Levels

- Level 1: Monitor and classify only.
- Level 2: Draft replies for approval.
- Level 3: Auto-send approved low-risk flows.

Recommendation: start at Level 2.

## Conversation Classes

- Scorecard request.
- Workflow pain.
- Qualified prospect.
- Beginner seller.
- Tool question.
- Pricing question.
- Compliance/risk question.
- Support issue.
- Spam/vendor pitch.
- Ambiguous.

## Approved Low-Risk Flows

- Scorecard request.
- What is Wholesale-Ops?
- Audit booking offer.

## Human Approval Required For

- Pricing.
- Amazon account health.
- IP complaints.
- Suspensions.
- Legal/compliance advice.
- Angry users.
- Refunds.
- Billing.
- Custom integrations.
- Profit guarantee questions.

## Workflow

1. Classify the conversation.
2. Draft a reply.
3. Request approval unless the flow is explicitly approved for auto-send.
4. Log class, reply, next step, and learning.
5. Escalate sensitive conversations immediately.

## DM Triage Process

`[New DM] --> [Classify] --> [Qualify] --> [Reply] --> [Log] --> [Handoff]`

1. Identify conversation class.
2. Check whether the person appears to be an Amazon FBA wholesale seller.
3. Ask one qualification question if unclear.
4. Use `instagram/dm-response-library.md` for safe replies.
5. Route risky topics to human approval.
6. Log workflow pain, objection, and next step.

## Response Timing Expectations

- Scorecard requests: respond same day, ideally within 2 business hours.
- Workflow pain DMs: respond same day with one qualification question.
- Qualified prospect DMs: route to workflow audit within 24 hours.
- Pricing, demo, integration, account-health, or IP questions: acknowledge and route to human review.
- Angry or sensitive DMs: do not improvise; escalate.

## High-Risk Escalation

Human approval required for:

- Pricing.
- Account health.
- IP complaints.
- Suspensions.
- Legal/compliance advice.
- Angry users.
- Refunds.
- Billing.
- Custom integrations.
- Profit guarantee questions.
- Seller Central integration questions.
- Product capability claims not documented.

## Scorecard Delivery Flow

1. User asks for scorecard or comments "scorecard."
2. Confirm fit: "Are you currently doing FBA wholesale with supplier catalogs or buy lists?"
3. If yes, send scorecard link.
4. Ask: "Which workflow feels messiest right now?"
5. Log response in `voice-of-customer/` or `analytics/research-insights-log.md`.
6. If pain is active and specific, offer workflow audit.

## Audit Booking Handoff

Route to Sales Enablement when:

- Seller uses VAs or sourcing team.
- Seller reviews supplier catalogs.
- Buy-list decisions happen in spreadsheets.
- Seller mentions workflow pain.
- Seller asks for demo.
- Seller asks what to fix first and has active operations.

## Objection Logging

Log objections daily:

- "I use sheets."
- "My VA handles this."
- "Does this find products?"
- "Does this guarantee profit?"
- "Can this help with account health?"
- "How much is it?"
- "Do you integrate with Seller Central?"
- "I am not big enough."

Send repeated objections to:

- Product Marketing Agent.
- Audience Research Agent.
- Analytics Agent.
- QA Agent if risky.
