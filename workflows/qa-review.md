# QA Review

Run on everything external. Fail on any item.

1. Facts: prices, offers, location, email, and the RMIAN 15% line match `product/brand-brief.md` and the live site.
2. No invented press, galleries, shows, testimonials, metrics, clients, or stories.
3. No commission price, deposit, or timeline stated publicly.
4. Voice: first person where the artist speaks, no banned words, no hype or false scarcity.
5. Permission confirmed for any person, buyer, commission, or reposted photo.
6. Community and RMIAN language reviewed; the artist speaks only for herself.
7. One CTA and the right link.
8. Alt text present.
