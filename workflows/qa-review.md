# QA Review

Use this process for all content, outbound, sales, website, and DM copy. QA is not a grammar pass. It is the control that keeps Wholesale-Ops focused on Amazon FBA wholesale operators and prevents unsupported claims.

## Required Review Order

`[Audience Fit] --> [Claim Safety] --> [Workflow Specificity] --> [CTA] --> [Evidence Log]`

## Audience Fit Checks

- Does it clearly target Amazon FBA wholesale sellers?
- Does it mention supplier outreach, catalogs, ASIN review, buy lists, POs, FBA shipments, replenishment, margins, VA workflows, or account-risk hygiene?
- Does it accidentally sound like brick-and-mortar wholesaling?
- Does it accidentally sound like traditional distributor software?
- Does it attract retail stores, warehouse operators, POS buyers, or distributor ERP buyers?

## Claim Safety Checks

- Does it make unsupported claims?
- Does it imply guaranteed profit?
- Does it imply Wholesale-Ops finds profitable products for the seller?
- Does it imply Amazon account issues can be prevented?
- Does it overpromise compliance, account-health, suspension, IP complaint, gated-category, or legal protection?
- Does it mention integrations, automations, customers, testimonials, metrics, or case studies that are not documented?
- Does it avoid get-rich-quick, passive-income, and hype language?

## Workflow Specificity Checks

- Is the copy tied to a real FBA wholesale workflow?
- Is the pain specific enough for an operator to recognize?
- Does it preserve decision history, ownership, status, assumptions, or handoff context?
- Does it explain the next step without feature dumping?

## CTA Checks

- Is the CTA clear?
- Is the CTA matched to intent: scorecard for low intent, workflow audit for high intent, conversation for research?
- Does the CTA avoid pressure or exaggerated urgency?

## Review Status

- Approved: ready to use.
- Revise: useful but needs edits before use.
- Blocked: wrong audience, unsupported claim, risky compliance/account-health language, invented feature, or unclear CTA.

## Severity

▲ high
BLOCK:
- Profit guarantees.
- Account-protection promises.
- Legal/compliance advice.
- Fake customers, metrics, testimonials, or case studies.
- Invented integrations or features.

REVISE:
- Vague SaaS language.
- Generic agency tone.
- Weak FBA wholesale specificity.
- CTA mismatch.

APPROVE:
- Practical, specific, evidence-aware operator language.
▼ low

## Evidence Standard

Label claims as one of:

- Documented: supported by product docs in this repo.
- Observed: supported by seller language, interviews, replies, or analytics.
- Hypothesis: useful but not yet validated.
- Blocked: unsafe or unsupported.

## Escalation

Human review is required for pricing, Amazon account health, IP complaints, suspensions, legal/compliance advice, refunds, billing, custom integrations, profit guarantee questions, and any claim about Amazon policy interpretation.
