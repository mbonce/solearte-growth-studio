# Content Production

1. Artist drops footage and photos (timelapse, hands, finished pieces, sneakers on feet) into the shared folder.
2. Content Agent matches to the calendar and pillar ratio.
3. Draft: hook, caption, CTA, alt text, design spec.
4. QA: facts, permission, voice, one CTA.
5. Artist approves or edits.
6. Schedule and post; reply to comments in the first hour.
7. Capture metrics after 48 hours.
