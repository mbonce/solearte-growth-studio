# Content Production Workflow

Instagram is the default first channel. Other channels should repurpose from Instagram unless the task says otherwise.

## Inputs

- Product positioning.
- Instagram content strategy.
- Instagram visual style guide.
- Content pillars.
- Customer language.
- Weekly growth theme.
- Last week’s content performance.

## Process

1. Select one FBA wholesale workflow pain.
2. Choose the Instagram-native format first: carousel, reel, story, single-image post, meme, or DM prompt.
3. Draft practical operator-focused content.
4. Repurpose the strongest idea into X, YouTube Shorts/Reels, Discord/private community prompts, or LinkedIn only when useful.
5. Add CTA to scorecard, workflow audit, or conversation.
6. Run QA before publishing.

## Outputs

- Draft content.
- Instagram-first campaign notes.
- QA notes.
- Published asset log.
- Performance entry.

## Review

- Does it help an FBA wholesale operator think more clearly?
- Is the claim supportable?
- Does it avoid generic SaaS and agency language?
