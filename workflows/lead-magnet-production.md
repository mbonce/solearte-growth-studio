# Lead Magnet Production Workflow

## Inputs

- Scorecard concept.
- Seller pain points.
- Product positioning.
- QA rules.

## Process

1. Pick one operating pain or diagnostic framework.
2. Draft the asset with practical questions.
3. Create a landing page and follow-up email.
4. QA for unsupported claims.
5. Track downloads and replies.

## Outputs

- Lead magnet draft.
- Landing page copy.
- Follow-up email.
- Analytics entry.

## Review

- Does it diagnose workflow pain?
- Does it naturally route to an audit?
- Does it avoid promising outcomes?
