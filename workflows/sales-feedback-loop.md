# Sales Feedback Loop

## Inputs

- Discovery calls.
- Workflow audits.
- Demo notes.
- Objections.
- Pilot outcomes.

## Process

1. Capture seller language immediately after calls.
2. Tag objections by category.
3. Identify repeated workflow pains.
4. Update product marketing and sales assets.
5. Feed themes to content and outbound.

## Outputs

- Updated objection handling.
- Customer language snippets.
- Product feedback.
- Next content angles.

## Review

- Is the learning from a qualified seller?
- Is it repeated or one-off?
- Does it change messaging, product, or sales process?
