# Analytics Reporting Workflow

## Inputs

- Content performance.
- Outbound performance.
- Instagram DM performance.
- Demo funnel.
- Experiment log.
- Learning log.

## Process

1. Collect weekly numbers.
2. Capture qualitative replies.
3. Identify repeated pain patterns.
4. Decide what to repeat, change, or stop.
5. Propose next week’s experiments.

## Outputs

- Weekly growth report.
- Experiment decisions.
- Updated learning log.

## Review

- Are numbers and interpretation separated?
- Are next actions tied to evidence?
- Are weak signals labeled as weak?
