# Weekly Growth Cycle

## Monday: Research And Strategy

- Agents involved: orchestrator, customer research, product marketing.
- Inputs: last week’s report, seller replies, DMs, interview notes, market questions.
- Outputs: weekly theme, pain pattern, priority segment, asset plan.
- Review checklist: target is FBA wholesale; pain is operational; claims are supportable.

## Tuesday: Content Production

- Agents involved: ops content, product marketing, QA.
- Inputs: content pillars, customer language, weekly theme.
- Outputs: LinkedIn posts, X posts, long-form draft, short-form scripts, carousel outline.
- Review checklist: practical, specific, no hype, clear CTA.

## Wednesday: Lead Magnet And Outbound

- Agents involved: outbound, Instagram DM, customer research, QA.
- Inputs: scorecard, prospecting criteria, sequences, content engagement.
- Outputs: outbound batch, DM drafts, scorecard follow-ups.
- Review checklist: plain copy, correct audience, no risky claims.

## Thursday: Sales Asset Improvement

- Agents involved: sales enablement, product marketing, account risk, QA.
- Inputs: sales calls, objections, audit notes, demo feedback.
- Outputs: updated scripts, objections, one-pager, pilot proposal notes.
- Review checklist: workflow-first demo, no feature dumping, clear poor-fit exits.

## Friday: Analytics And Feedback

- Agents involved: analytics, orchestrator, QA.
- Inputs: performance trackers, replies, audits, demos, pilot notes.
- Outputs: weekly report, learning log, next experiments.
- Review checklist: evidence separated from interpretation, next actions clear.
