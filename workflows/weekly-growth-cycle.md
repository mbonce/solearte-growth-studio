# Weekly Growth Cycle

| Day | Focus | Owner | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Review last week, capture research, set priorities | Orchestrator, Audience Research | `agent-ops/task-queue.md` |
| Tue | Produce the week's posts from footage and finished work | Instagram Content | content queue |
| Wed | Commission follow-ups, collab outreach, shop pushes | Commission Sales, Collab Outreach, Shop | pipeline updates |
| Thu | Improve one sales asset or site page | Shop, Brand Narrative | `website/`, `sales/` |
| Fri | Weekly report, learning, QA of next week | Analytics, QA | `analytics/weekly-growth-report.md` |

Artist time needed: roughly 2–3 hours per week for footage, approvals, and proposals.
