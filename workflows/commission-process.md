# Commission Process

1. Inquiry arrives (DM, email, form, in person). Log same day in `analytics/commission-pipeline.md`.
2. Reply with the inquiry script; gather subject, size, references, timing, placement. Move to email.
3. Artist reviews and decides scope, price, timing.
4. Send proposal from `sales/commission-proposal-template.md`.
5. Yes --> deposit --> start. Not yet --> one follow-up, then hold.
6. Progress updates if the client wants them.
7. Deliver with `sales/commission-delivery-checklist.md`.
8. Ask permission to share; capture quotes.
