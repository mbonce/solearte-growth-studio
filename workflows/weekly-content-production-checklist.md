# Weekly Content Production Checklist

## Weekly Goal

Feed Instagram-first acquisition with proof-oriented operational content and scorecard pathways.

## Monday – Research-Led Planning

- [ ] Pull top 3 recurring pain phrases from `voice-of-customer/direct-quotes.md`.
- [ ] Update hook opportunities in `creative-research/high-performing-hooks.md`.
- [ ] Select 3 content posts aligned with active pain clusters.
- [ ] Confirm scorecard CTA appears in every first draft.

## Tuesday – Draft Assets

- [ ] Draft 2 carousels or 1 reel + 1 story sequence.
- [ ] Draft comment prompts and one DM qualification question per post.
- [ ] Send first-round drafts to QA.

## Wednesday – Review and Queue

- [ ] QA check for:
  - FBA-only framing
  - no promise language
  - scorecard-first CTA
- [ ] Queue tomorrow’s post and story in `launch/approval-board.md`.
- [ ] Confirm first-hour response plan.

## Thursday – Publish Preparation

- [ ] Export finalized assets.
- [ ] Confirm scorecard link and booking link are correct placeholders.
- [ ] Ensure first-day founder review fields are pre-filled.

## Friday – Learn and Repurpose

- [ ] Review week metrics.
- [ ] Tag best-performing hooks in `analytics/weekly-growth-report.md`.
- [ ] Capture repurpose ideas for X or founder-level posts.
- [ ] Create next week’s list from pain-to-pipeline opportunities.
