# Workflow Audit Process

## Inputs

- Discovery notes.
- Seller tools.
- One selected workflow.
- Example sheet, catalog, buy list, PO, shipment, or replenishment decision.

## Process

1. Confirm FBA wholesale fit.
2. Map the current workflow.
3. Identify owner, status, decision, handoff, and review gaps.
4. Score pain and operational risk.
5. Recommend cleanup or disqualify if too early.

## Outputs

- Workflow map.
- Bottleneck list.
- Pilot fit recommendation.
- Follow-up notes.

## Review

- Is the scope one real workflow?
- Are success criteria operational?
- Are unsupported promises removed?
