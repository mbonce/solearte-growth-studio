# Denver Art Scene

Working map of where a Denver contemporary artist shows up. Verify dates and details before acting; this is a starting list, not a calendar.

## Districts and moments to research

- First Friday art walks in Denver's art districts.
- Maker and art markets across the year.
- Neighborhood businesses near 80211 that host local artists.
- Mural programs and wall opportunities.
- Cultural festivals tied to immigrant and diaspora communities.
- Sneaker and streetwear events for Carry Your Colors.

## Venue types

| Type | Why it fits | Ask |
|---|---|---|
| Cafés and restaurants | Wall space, daily foot traffic, local pride | Rotating show with inquiry cards |
| Co-working and creative spaces | Buyers with walls and budgets | Show plus artist talk |
| Community centers and nonprofits | Story resonance, RMIAN adjacency | Live painting, donated piece for auction |
| Boutiques and sneaker shops | Carry Your Colors placement | Small consignment or pop-up |
| Event organizers | Live painting and portraits on site | Fee plus commission leads |
