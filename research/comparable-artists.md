## Patterns to look for

- How they open a commission conversation and whether they publish prices.
- How they show process (timelapse, hands, layers, before/after).
- How they talk about a cause without letting it replace the work.
- How they present product photography for wearable pieces.

## Where SOLEARTé is different by default

- Portraits and wearable art under one story, with a documented 15% RMIAN commitment.
- First-person immigrant story told by the artist herself.
