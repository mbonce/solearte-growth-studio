# Supplier Follow-Up Validation Brief

Date: 2026-06-10

Status: internal-only research brief.

Confidence at start: Low.

Purpose: keep supplier follow-up in a validation lane until stronger first-person evidence exists.

## Current Assessment

- Supplier follow-up is plausible as a workflow pain.
- The current evidence mix is too weak to treat it as a headline claim.
- The strongest existing signals are advice/content marketing, marketplace positioning, and exploratory forum prompts rather than direct seller complaints.

## What Counts As Stronger Evidence

Promote supplier follow-up from Low to Medium only if at least one of these is captured:

1. Three or more first-person seller/operator complaints about missed follow-ups, unclear owner, lost terms, or stalled approvals.
2. Two or more workflow examples that connect follow-up failure to buying delays, missed approvals, or duplicated work.
3. Repeated wording that shows where the next action actually gets lost: inbox, spreadsheet, CRM, VA handoff, or notes.

## Research Questions

- Where do wholesale sellers track supplier follow-up today: spreadsheet, inbox, CRM, task board, or VA notes?
- What exactly gets lost: status, owner, terms, reorder timing, next step, or last-touch history?
- Is the pain strongest before supplier approval, after approval, or during reorder/requote cycles?
- Do operators describe this as a lead-tracking problem, a relationship-management problem, or a buying-workflow problem?

## Safe Search Plan

- Search for direct complaint threads, not advice articles first.
- Prioritize first-person operator language over vendor/product pages.
- Capture exact phrasing only when the source really contains it; otherwise summarize and label it as a summary.

## Claim Guardrails

- Do not claim supplier follow-up is a common top pain yet.
- Do not claim it causes account-health, compliance, or legal issues.
- Do not promise that better tracking increases profit, prevents stockouts, or improves supplier relationships unless those claims are directly documented and approved.

## Output Contract

For each future evidence row, capture:

- Source and date.
- Exact quote or clearly labeled summary.
- Workflow moment: prospecting, approval, reorder, or ongoing relationship management.
- What was lost: owner, status, terms, next action, or timing.
- Confidence tier and whether it is safe for internal messaging only.

## Decision Rule

- If stronger direct evidence appears, update `research/evidence-ledger.md`, `analytics/research-insights-log.md`, and the task queue.
- If evidence stays weak, keep supplier follow-up as a secondary diagnostic angle only and continue leading with spreadsheet/workflow load plus VA review-state pain.
