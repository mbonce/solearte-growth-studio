## Executive Summary

Nova Analytics is positioned as a near real-time Amazon seller analytics platform for FBA/FBM sellers, brands, agencies, aggregators, and Amazon data teams. Its public product story centers on SKU-level profit accuracy, hourly refresh, ad/PPC analytics, inventory risk, custom reporting, and a large SEO/content footprint around Amazon metrics and seller operations.

The French legal entity tied to the service is NDA Nova Data Analytics, registered with RCS Lyon under SIREN 978 950 350. The French government business directory lists NOVA DATA ANALYTICS (NDA) as created on August 29, 2023, while the domain novadata.io itself was registered much earlier, on August 15, 2014, according to Identity Digital RDAP.

Public website traffic is modest but meaningful for a young vertical SaaS/content play: Similarweb estimated 38.1K total visits in May 2026, down 18.81% month over month, with 38.34% bounce rate, 2.06 pages per visit, and 53 seconds average visit duration. Nova's sitemap exposes roughly 1,193 public URLs, suggesting an aggressive SEO strategy around calculators, KPI-library pages, blog/news, directories, and comparison content.

## Product Positioning

## Key Diligence Notes

1. Company registration is clear: French public registry shows creation on August 29, 2023.
2. Domain history is older: novadata.io was registered August 15, 2014, so do not infer company age from domain age.
3. Marketplace count is inconsistent: public pages say both 21 and 23 marketplaces. I would verify this in a product demo or directly with Nova before using the count in collateral.
4. Founder/team naming is not perfectly clean across public sources. The French registry/Pappers currently point to Antoine Huet as president; LinkedIn shows Antoine Huet, Maxime Huet, and Matthieu Auger profiles; Nova pages emphasize a small product/engineering/GTM team. Avoid repeating unverified founder lists without confirmation.
5. Traffic is estimated, not actual. Similarweb's 38.1K visits is useful directionally, but not an audited metric.
6. The content footprint is real. Over 1,100 sitemap URLs, explicit AI crawler allowances, KPI libraries, calculators, directories, comparison pages, and news coverage all point to a serious SEO/GEO acquisition strategy.
