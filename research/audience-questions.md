# Audience Questions

Open questions the growth studio must answer with real conversations, DMs, comments, and sales.

## Commissioners

- What occasion triggers the first inquiry most often: milestone, memorial, gift, or self-portrait?
- What do they send as references, and what do they worry about when sending them?
- Do they want to see progress, or only the finished piece?
- What phrase do they use for the style: "colorful," "bold," "expressive," "abstract," "like a painting of us"?

## Collectors

- Where did they first see the work: Instagram, in person, a friend's wall?
- What made them stop scrolling?
- Size and price comfort for a first original?
- Do they care about Denver origin, the immigrant story, or the color first?

## Carry Your Colors

- Who buys the sneakers: for themselves or as a gift?
- Does the RMIAN 15% change the decision, the share, or neither?
- What sizing and shipping questions come up?
- Which piece do people ask for that does not exist yet (tee, tote, print, hat)?

## Collaborators

- What do venues and brands ask for first: a mural, live painting, product art, or a talk?
- What budget language do they use?
- What do they want to say about the artist, and is it accurate?

## Method

Every answer gets logged in `research/interview-notes.md` or `voice-of-customer/direct-quotes.md` with date and source.
