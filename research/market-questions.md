# Market Questions

## ICP

- How many suppliers or catalogs create enough pain to pay?
- Does the strongest urgency come from founders, VA managers, or replenishment owners?
- Which sellers are too early for this product?

## Workflow

- Which workflow breaks first: supplier pipeline, catalog review, buy list, PO/shipment, replenishment, or VA accountability?
- What fields do sellers already track manually?
- What decision history is most painful to reconstruct?

## Buying

- Is a scorecard enough to generate qualified audits?
- Do sellers prefer a pilot, audit, or direct SaaS subscription?
- What makes the pilot feel concrete enough to buy?

## Messaging

- Does "operations command center" land with qualified sellers?
- Does "spreadsheet chaos" attract the right audience or too many early sellers?
- Which content pillar creates the best seller replies?
