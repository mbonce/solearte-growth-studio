# Community Research

Communities where the work and the story resonate. Approach as a member and neighbor, never as a marketer.

## Denver and Front Range

- Immigrant and first-generation communities across Denver, including the neighborhoods around 80211 (Highland, Sunnyside, Berkeley).
- Latino, Black, Asian, and African diaspora arts and cultural organizations.
- Immigrant-serving nonprofits, starting with RMIAN (already supported through Carry Your Colors).
- Denver art walks, First Friday districts, maker markets, and small galleries.
- Creative co-working spaces, coffee shops, and restaurants that hang local work.

## Online

- Instagram communities around figurative and portrait painting, color, and diaspora art.
- First-generation and immigrant storytelling accounts.
- Sneaker-culture and custom-kicks communities for Carry Your Colors.
- Denver local accounts and event listings.

## How to show up

1. Comment as the artist, in her voice, about the work and the shared story.
2. Offer, do not pitch: live painting, a talk, a piece for an auction, a collaboration.
3. Log every real conversation in `research/interview-notes.md`.

## Boundaries

- Do not speak for any community. Share her story, invite others to share theirs.
- Do not use RMIAN's name beyond the documented 15% commitment without their agreement.
