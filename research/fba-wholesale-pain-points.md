# FBA Wholesale Pain Points

## Supplier Pipeline

- Supplier targets live in scattered spreadsheets.
- Follow-ups are missed or duplicated.
- Approval status, terms, and contacts are unclear.

## Catalog Analysis

- Catalog files are reviewed inconsistently.
- ASIN matches and decisions are not documented.
- Old catalogs are forgotten instead of revisited.
- Public-source evidence supports supplier-sheet intake as a medium-confidence pain lane: sellers and practitioner sources describe supplier spreadsheets, price lists, catalogs, CSV/XLSX files, UPC/EAN/ASIN matching, and manual review workflows.
- Keep claims diagnostic: supplier sheets create review workflow load; do not imply automated buying decisions or guaranteed profitability.

## Buy-List Quality

- Approved products move into buy lists without clear reasoning.
- Operators cannot quickly see who approved an ASIN or why.
- VAs may optimize for volume instead of decision quality.

## Margin And ROI

- Fee changes, prep costs, inbound shipping, storage, returns, and repricing can erode margin.
- Sellers often rely on static assumptions.
- Replenishment decisions may use stale numbers.

## PO And Shipment Workflow

- POs, invoices, shipment plans, prep status, and receiving are hard to reconcile.
- Exceptions get handled in messages instead of workflow records.

## VA Workflow

- Work quality depends on private notes and individual discipline.
- Review states are unclear.
- Operators lack visibility into bottlenecks.
- Public hiring posts support VA/team handoff as a medium-confidence pain lane: managers ask for clean Google Sheets, ASIN matching, eligibility/profitability checks, pass/fail decisions, and ready-to-buy flags.
- Needs more first-person operator evidence before promoting to high confidence.

## Risk Control

- Invoices, authorization notes, restrictions, IP concerns, and account-health context are not centralized.
- Risk review may happen too late in the buy decision.
