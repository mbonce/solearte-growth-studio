# Collector Language

How people talk about wanting and owning expressive portrait work. Seeded from the artist's own site language; extend only with captured quotes.

## From the site (artist's words)

- "saturated contemporary portraiture"
- "rooted in identity, resilience, and movement"
- "translate lived experience into color, gesture, and work meant to be carried forward"
- "movement is natural"
- "the courage it takes to begin again"
- "stepping forward without leaving behind where you come from"
- "unapologetic individuality"

## Likely buyer phrases (hypotheses, verify)

- "I want something that actually looks like us, but alive."
- "The colors are insane." / "I can't stop looking at it."
- "I want to give my mom something that means something."
- "I've never bought real art before."
- "Where would I even hang it?"
- "I saw myself in it."

## Phrases to build on once confirmed

- "carry" and "carried" (the collection name already does this work)
- "begin again"
- "my colors" / "our colors"

## Phrases to avoid

- "investment," "asset," "appreciate in value"
- "affordable art," "budget-friendly"
- "exclusive," "VIP," "elite"
